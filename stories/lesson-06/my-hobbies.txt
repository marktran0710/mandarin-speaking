《我的興趣》My Hobbies
Situation: A student describes a close friend group, their shared love of Chinese, music, singing, and weekend sports.
Source: Lesson 6 – 週末去打網球吧！ (短文 Reading, track 06-3), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's 短文《我的興趣》verbatim; pinyin and English
are the book's own, from the provided Lesson 6 reading packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: four college-age friends gathered around a small table in a bright classroom lounge — open Chinese notebooks, headphones, a small microphone, and a shared music player show their common hobbies]
Narrator：我有幾個好朋友，我們都喜歡學中文、聽音樂、唱歌。
Wǒ yǒu jǐ ge hǎo péngyǒu, wǒmen dōu xǐhuān xué Zhōngwén, tīng yīnyuè, chànggē.
I have some good friends. We all like to learn Chinese, listen to music and sing.

[Turn 2 — scene note: 中明 standing near the classroom lounge window with a microphone, happily singing while the other friends listen and clap]
Narrator：中明最愛唱歌，他的中文歌唱得很好。
Zhōngmíng zuì ài chànggē, tā de Zhōngwén gē chàng de hěn hǎo.
Zhongming likes to sing the most, he sings Chinese songs well.

[Turn 3 — scene note: weekend at a recreation center — 宜文 swimming in a pool lane, 國安 dribbling a basketball, the narrator holding a tennis racket, and the friends also watching a sports game together from nearby seats]
Narrator：平常我們都有點兒忙，可是週末我們常一起去運動，宜文游泳游得不錯，國安的籃球打得很好，我喜歡打網球，有時候我們也一起去看運動比賽。
Píngcháng wǒmen dōu yǒudiǎnr máng, kěshì zhōumò wǒmen cháng yìqǐ qù yùndòng, Yíwén yóuyǒng yóu de búcuò, Guó'ān de lánqiú dǎ de hěn hǎo, wǒ xǐhuān dǎ wǎngqiú, yǒushíhòu wǒmen yě yìqǐ qù kàn yùndòng bǐsài.
Usually we are all a bit busy, but we go exercise often on weekends. Yiwen is good at swimming; Guoan plays basketball very well; I enjoy playing tennis. Sometimes we watch sport games together as well.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the four friends sitting together after exercise, smiling with Chinese notebooks, rackets, a basketball, and swim gear beside them]
Narrator：有這幾個好朋友，我真高興！
Yǒu zhè jǐ ge hǎo péngyǒu, wǒ zhēn gāoxìng!
I am so glad to have these great friends!

Vocabulary
1  興趣      xìngqù       N      interest; hobby
2  幾        jǐ           N      several; a few
3  學/學習   xué/xuéxí    V      to learn; to study
4  唱歌      chànggē      V-sep  to sing (a song)
5  歌        gē           N      song (M:首 shǒu)
6  平常      píngcháng    N      generally; usually
7  有(的)時候  yǒu(de)shíhòu  Adv  sometimes
8  比賽      bǐsài        N      competition; race; game
9  真        zhēn         Adv    really
10 高興      gāoxìng      Vs     happy

Key Phrases
1  我有幾個 + N。  —  我有幾個好朋友。  —  Say you have several of something or a small group of people.
2  都喜歡 + V。  —  我們都喜歡學中文、聽音樂、唱歌。  —  Use 都 to say everyone in a group likes the same activities.
3  V 得很好。  —  他的中文歌唱得很好。  —  Describe how well someone does an action; 得 links the action to the evaluation.
4  有時候…也…  —  有時候我們也一起去看運動比賽。  —  Add an occasional extra activity to a usual routine.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: four college-age friends gathered around a small table in a bright classroom lounge — open Chinese notebooks, headphones, a small microphone, and a shared music player show their common hobbies]
Narrator：我有幾個很好的朋友，我們平常都喜歡一起學中文、聽音樂，也喜歡唱歌。
Wǒ yǒu jǐ ge hěn hǎo de péngyǒu, wǒmen píngcháng dōu xǐhuān yìqǐ xué Zhōngwén, tīng yīnyuè, yě xǐhuān chànggē.
I have several very good friends. We usually like studying Chinese together, listening to music, and singing too.

[Turn 2 — scene note: 中明 standing near the classroom lounge window with a microphone, happily singing while the other friends listen and clap]
Narrator：中明最愛唱中文歌，他唱得很自然，也唱得很好聽。
Zhōngmíng zuì ài chàng Zhōngwén gē, tā chàng de hěn zìrán, yě chàng de hěn hǎotīng.
Zhongming loves singing Chinese songs the most. He sings naturally, and he sounds very good.

[Turn 3 — scene note: weekend at a recreation center — 宜文 swimming in a pool lane, 國安 dribbling a basketball, the narrator holding a tennis racket, and the friends also watching a sports game together from nearby seats]
Narrator：平常我們都有點兒忙，可是週末常一起去運動；宜文游泳游得不錯，國安籃球打得很好，我最喜歡打網球，有時候也跟他們去看運動比賽。
Píngcháng wǒmen dōu yǒudiǎnr máng, kěshì zhōumò cháng yìqǐ qù yùndòng; Yíwén yóuyǒng yóu de búcuò, Guó'ān lánqiú dǎ de hěn hǎo, wǒ zuì xǐhuān dǎ wǎngqiú, yǒushíhòu yě gēn tāmen qù kàn yùndòng bǐsài.
Usually we are all a little busy, but on weekends we often exercise together. Yiwen swims pretty well, Guoan plays basketball very well, I like tennis the most, and sometimes I also go watch sports games with them.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the four friends sitting together after exercise, smiling with Chinese notebooks, rackets, a basketball, and swim gear beside them]
Narrator：有這幾個朋友一起學習、一起運動，我真的很高興！
Yǒu zhè jǐ ge péngyǒu yìqǐ xuéxí, yìqǐ yùndòng, wǒ zhēnde hěn gāoxìng!
Having these friends to study and exercise with makes me really happy!

Vocabulary
1  一起      yìqǐ       Adv   together
2  自然      zìrán      Vs    natural
3  好聽      hǎotīng    Vs    pleasant to listen to; good-sounding
4  跟        gēn        Prep  with
5  真的      zhēnde     Adv   really; truly
6  學習      xuéxí      V     to learn; to study

Key Phrases
1  平常都喜歡…  —  我們平常都喜歡一起學中文、聽音樂，也喜歡唱歌。  —  Describe what a group usually likes doing.
2  V 得很 Adj，也 V 得很 Adj。  —  他唱得很自然，也唱得很好聽。  —  Give two simple evaluations of the same action.
3  最喜歡 + V。  —  我最喜歡打網球。  —  Name your favourite activity inside a larger set of hobbies.
4  跟 + 人 + 去 + V。  —  有時候也跟他們去看運動比賽。  —  Say who you go with when you do an activity.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: four college-age friends gathered around a small table in a bright classroom lounge — open Chinese notebooks, headphones, a small microphone, and a shared music player show their common hobbies]
Narrator：我有幾個興趣差不多的好朋友；除了學中文以外，我們也常一起聽音樂、練唱歌。
Wǒ yǒu jǐ ge xìngqù chàbuduō de hǎo péngyǒu; chúle xué Zhōngwén yǐwài, wǒmen yě cháng yìqǐ tīng yīnyuè, liàn chànggē.
I have several good friends with similar interests. Besides studying Chinese, we also often listen to music and practice singing together.

[Turn 2 — scene note: 中明 standing near the classroom lounge window with a microphone, happily singing while the other friends listen and clap]
Narrator：中明比我們都愛唱歌，中文歌唱得又清楚又好聽。
Zhōngmíng bǐ wǒmen dōu ài chànggē, Zhōngwén gē chàng de yòu qīngchǔ yòu hǎotīng.
Zhongming loves singing more than any of us, and he sings Chinese songs both clearly and beautifully.

[Turn 3 — scene note: weekend at a recreation center — 宜文 swimming in a pool lane, 國安 dribbling a basketball, the narrator holding a tennis racket, and the friends also watching a sports game together from nearby seats]
Narrator：雖然平常大家都有點兒忙，可是到了週末，我們常約好一起去運動；宜文游泳游得比以前好，國安籃球打得很穩，我打網球打得最開心，有時候我們還會一起去看運動比賽。
Suīrán píngcháng dàjiā dōu yǒudiǎnr máng, kěshì dào le zhōumò, wǒmen cháng yuē hǎo yìqǐ qù yùndòng; Yíwén yóuyǒng yóu de bǐ yǐqián hǎo, Guó'ān lánqiú dǎ de hěn wěn, wǒ dǎ wǎngqiú dǎ de zuì kāixīn, yǒushíhòu wǒmen hái huì yìqǐ qù kàn yùndòng bǐsài.
Although everyone is usually a little busy, when the weekend comes we often arrange to exercise together. Yiwen swims better than before, Guoan plays basketball steadily, I feel happiest playing tennis, and sometimes we also go watch sports games together.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the four friends sitting together after exercise, smiling with Chinese notebooks, rackets, a basketball, and swim gear beside them]
Narrator：能有這幾個好朋友，跟他們一起學習、運動，我覺得真高興！
Néng yǒu zhè jǐ ge hǎo péngyǒu, gēn tāmen yìqǐ xuéxí, yùndòng, wǒ juéde zhēn gāoxìng!
Being able to have these good friends, and to study and exercise with them, makes me feel truly happy!

Vocabulary
1  差不多    chàbuduō    Vs    about the same; similar
2  除了…以外  chúle…yǐwài  Pat   besides; apart from
3  練        liàn        V     to practice
4  比        bǐ          Prep  compared with; than
5  清楚      qīngchǔ     Vs    clear
6  大家      dàjiā       N     everyone
7  約好      yuē hǎo     V     to arrange; to agree on
8  以前      yǐqián      N     before; in the past
9  穩        wěn         Vs    steady
10 會        huì         Aux   will; be likely to
11 能        néng        Aux   can; be able to
12 覺得      juéde       Vst   to feel; to think

Key Phrases
1  除了…以外，也…  —  除了學中文以外，我們也常一起聽音樂、練唱歌。  —  Add another regular activity beyond the first one.
2  A 比 B 都 + V/Adj。  —  中明比我們都愛唱歌，中文歌唱得又清楚又好聽。  —  Say one person stands out from the whole group.
3  又 Adj 又 Adj。  —  中文歌唱得又清楚又好聽。  —  Give two positive qualities in a compact spoken line.
4  雖然…，可是…  —  雖然平常大家都有點兒忙，可是到了週末，我們常約好一起去運動。  —  Contrast a normal problem with what still happens.
5  我覺得真…  —  我覺得真高興！  —  Make the feeling clearly personal and sincere.
