《看電影還是騎腳踏車？》Movie or Bike Ride?
Situation: Guo'an invites Zhongming to see an interesting movie, but Zhongming feels tired and wants to rest; Guo'an suggests exercise, and they negotiate a bike ride for the weekend.
Source: Lesson 6 – 週末去打網球吧！Let's Play Tennis This Weekend! (Dialogue 2), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1) — the book's dialogue, verbatim
═══════════════════
(Textbook Dialogue 2; pinyin and English are copied from the provided
source packet.)

[Turn 1 — 校園門口的傍晚，國安拿著手機上的電影圖示，興奮地邀請中明]
國安：聽說這部電影很有趣，我們晚上一起去看，好不好？
Tīngshuō zhè bù diànyǐng hěn yǒuqù. Wǒmen wǎnshàng yìqǐ qù kàn, hǎo bù hǎo?
I heard that this movie is very interesting. How about we go to see it tonight?

[Turn 2 — 中明背著書包，手裡拿著一疊課本，揉著眼睛，看起來很累]
中明：我覺得有一點兒累，我想回家休息。
Wǒ juéde yǒu yìdiǎnr lèi, wǒ xiǎng huí jiā xiūxí.
I am feeling a bit tired, I want to go home for a rest.

[Turn 3 — 國安一手指著中明的手機，一手指向想像泡泡裡的明天空格日曆、腳踏車和公園]
國安：你不要常常在家上網、看電腦、玩手機，應該去運動。明天我有空，我們可以一起去騎腳踏車。
Nǐ búyào chángcháng zài jiā shàngwǎng, kàn diànnǎo, wán shǒujī, yīnggāi qù yùndòng. Míngtiān wǒ yǒukòng, wǒmen kěyǐ yìqǐ qù qí jiǎotàchē.
Don't surf the internet, use the computer or play on the cell phone at home often. You should go exercise. I am available tomorrow. We can go ride bikes together.

[Turn 4 — 中明抱著書包，想像泡泡裡有耳機和枕頭]
中明：可是我只想聽音樂、睡覺。
Kěshì wǒ zhǐ xiǎng tīng yīnyuè, shuìjiào.
But I only want to listen to music and sleep.

[Turn 5 — 國安伸出兩根手指，試著換一個時間]
國安：後天去呢？
Hòutiān qù ne?
How about the day after tomorrow then?

[Turn 6 — 中明看向吹風的樹，再指著週末去騎車的想像畫面]
中明：這兩天天氣有點兒冷，我們週末去，好嗎？
Zhè liǎng tiān tiānqì yǒudiǎnr lěng, wǒmen zhōumò qù, hǎo ma?
It is a bit cold those two days. How about we do it this weekend?

Vocabulary
1  電影      diànyǐng    N     movie
2  部        bù          M     measure word for movies
3  有趣      yǒuqù       Vs    interesting
4  覺得      juéde       Vst   to feel; to think
5  有一點兒  yǒu yìdiǎnr Adv   a little
6  休息      xiūxí       Vi    to take a rest
7  上網      shàngwǎng   V-sep to use/surf the internet
8  電腦      diànnǎo     N     computer
9  手機      shǒujī      N     cell phone
10 應該      yīnggāi     Vaux  should; ought to
11 運動      yùndòng     Vi    to exercise
12 可以      kěyǐ        Vaux  can; could
13 騎        qí          V     to ride
14 腳踏車    jiǎotàchē   N     bicycle; bike
15 只        zhǐ         Adv   only; just
16 後天      hòutiān     N     the day after tomorrow

Key Phrases
1  聽說...  —  聽說這部電影很有趣。  —  Introduce something you heard and want to talk about.
2  ...，好不好？  —  我們晚上一起去看，好不好？  —  Make a friendly invitation and ask for agreement.
3  不要...，應該...  —  你不要常常在家上網、看電腦、玩手機，應該去運動。  —  Tell someone what not to do, then suggest a better action.
4  可以一起 V  —  我們可以一起去騎腳踏車。  —  Suggest an activity that two people can do together.
5  ...呢？  —  後天去呢？  —  Offer another option after the first plan does not work.

MEDIUM (A1-A2) — same beats, richer word choice

[Turn 1 — 校園門口的傍晚，國安拿著手機上的電影圖示，興奮地邀請中明]
國安：聽說這部電影非常有趣，我們今天晚上一起去電影院看，好不好？
Tīngshuō zhè bù diànyǐng fēicháng yǒuqù, wǒmen jīntiān wǎnshàng yìqǐ qù diànyǐngyuàn kàn, hǎo bù hǎo?
I heard this movie is very interesting; how about we go to the movie theater tonight to see it together?

[Turn 2 — 中明背著書包，手裡拿著一疊課本，揉著眼睛，看起來很累]
中明：我覺得有一點兒累，今天想早一點回家休息。
Wǒ juéde yǒu yìdiǎnr lèi, jīntiān xiǎng zǎo yìdiǎn huí jiā xiūxí.
I feel a little tired and want to go home a bit early today to rest.

[Turn 3 — 國安一手指著中明的手機，一手指向想像泡泡裡的明天空格日曆、腳踏車和公園]
國安：你不要常常在家上網、看電腦、玩手機，應該多去運動。明天我有空，我們可以一起去騎腳踏車。
Nǐ búyào chángcháng zài jiā shàngwǎng, kàn diànnǎo, wán shǒujī, yīnggāi duō qù yùndòng. Míngtiān wǒ yǒukòng, wǒmen kěyǐ yìqǐ qù qí jiǎotàchē.
Don't often stay home surfing the internet, using the computer, and playing on your phone; you should exercise more. I'm free tomorrow, so we can go ride bikes together.

[Turn 4 — 中明抱著書包，想像泡泡裡有耳機和枕頭]
中明：可是我今天只想聽音樂、睡覺，不想出門。
Kěshì wǒ jīntiān zhǐ xiǎng tīng yīnyuè, shuìjiào, bù xiǎng chūmén.
But today I only want to listen to music and sleep; I don't want to go out.

[Turn 5 — 國安伸出兩根手指，試著換一個時間]
國安：那後天去呢？天氣可能比較好。
Nà hòutiān qù ne? Tiānqì kěnéng bǐjiào hǎo.
Then how about the day after tomorrow? The weather might be better.

[Turn 6 — 中明看向吹風的樹，再指著週末去騎車的想像畫面]
中明：這兩天天氣有點兒冷，我們週末再去，好嗎？
Zhè liǎng tiān tiānqì yǒudiǎnr lěng, wǒmen zhōumò zài qù, hǎo ma?
The weather is a little cold these two days; how about we go this weekend instead?

Vocabulary
1  非常      fēicháng     Adv   very; extremely
2  電影院    diànyǐngyuàn N     movie theater; cinema
3  今天      jīntiān      N     today
4  早一點    zǎo yìdiǎn   Ph    a bit earlier
5  多        duō          Adv   more; often
6  出門      chūmén       V-sep to go out
7  那        nà           Det   then; in that case
8  可能      kěnéng       Adv   possibly; might
9  比較      bǐjiào       Adv   comparatively; rather
10 再        zài          Adv   again; then; instead

Key Phrases
1  今天晚上...  —  我們今天晚上一起去電影院看，好不好？  —  Add a clear time to make a plan more concrete.
2  應該多 V  —  應該多去運動。  —  Suggest that someone do more of a healthy or useful action.
3  不想 V  —  不想出門。  —  Say what you do not feel like doing.
4  可能比較...  —  天氣可能比較好。  —  Make a soft guess about how something might be.
5  週末再 V  —  我們週末再去，好嗎？  —  Move a plan to the weekend instead of doing it now.

HARD (A2) — same beats, fullest word choice
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — 校園門口的傍晚，國安拿著手機上的電影圖示，興奮地邀請中明]
國安：聽說這部電影很有意思，我想看很久了，晚上一起去，好不好？
Tīngshuō zhè bù diànyǐng hěn yǒu yìsi, wǒ xiǎng kàn hěn jiǔ le, wǎnshàng yìqǐ qù, hǎo bù hǎo?
I heard this movie is really interesting, and I've wanted to see it for a long time; how about we go together tonight?

[Turn 2 — 中明背著書包，手裡拿著一疊課本，揉著眼睛，看起來很累]
中明：我覺得有點累，今天上了一天課，想先回家休息一下。
Wǒ juéde yǒudiǎn lèi, jīntiān shàng le yì tiān kè, xiǎng xiān huí jiā xiūxí yíxià.
I feel a little tired; I had class all day today and want to go home first to rest a bit.

[Turn 3 — 國安一手指著中明的手機，一手指向想像泡泡裡的明天空格日曆、腳踏車和公園]
國安：你不要老是在家上網、看電腦、玩手機，應該出去運動。明天我有空，一起騎腳踏車比一直在家好。
Nǐ búyào lǎoshì zài jiā shàngwǎng, kàn diànnǎo, wán shǒujī, yīnggāi chūqù yùndòng. Míngtiān wǒ yǒukòng, yìqǐ qí jiǎotàchē bǐ yìzhí zài jiā hǎo.
Don't always stay home surfing the internet, using the computer, and playing on your phone; you should go out and exercise. I'm free tomorrow, and riding bikes together is better than staying home all the time.

[Turn 4 — 中明抱著書包，想像泡泡裡有耳機和枕頭]
中明：可是我只想放音樂、早點睡，這樣比較舒服。
Kěshì wǒ zhǐ xiǎng fàng yīnyuè, zǎodiǎn shuì, zhèyàng bǐjiào shūfu.
But I only want to put on some music and sleep early; that feels more comfortable.

[Turn 5 — 國安伸出兩根手指，試著換一個時間]
國安：那後天呢？如果天氣好，我們就去。
Nà hòutiān ne? Rúguǒ tiānqì hǎo, wǒmen jiù qù.
Then what about the day after tomorrow? If the weather is good, we'll go then.

[Turn 6 — 中明看向吹風的樹，再指著週末去騎車的想像畫面]
中明：這兩天天氣有點冷，我怕騎車不舒服，週末天氣比較暖，我們那時候去，好嗎？
Zhè liǎng tiān tiānqì yǒudiǎn lěng, wǒ pà qí chē bù shūfu, zhōumò tiānqì bǐjiào nuǎn, wǒmen nà shíhòu qù, hǎo ma?
The weather is a little cold these two days, and I'm afraid riding won't be comfortable; the weekend will be warmer, so how about we go then?

Key Phrases
1  想 V 很久了  —  我想看很久了。  —  Say you have wanted to do something for a long time.
2  V 了一天 N  —  今天上了一天課。  —  Explain why you are tired by saying you did something all day.
3  A 比 B + Vs  —  一起騎腳踏車比一直在家好。  —  Compare two options directly with 比.
4  如果...，就...  —  如果天氣好，我們就去。  —  State a simple condition and what you will do if it happens.
5  怕...不舒服  —  我怕騎車不舒服。  —  Explain a concern before suggesting another plan.
