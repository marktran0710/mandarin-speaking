《週末去打網球吧！》Let's Play Tennis This Weekend!
Situation: Guoan, Youmei, and Yiwen are deciding what activity to do together on a sunny weekend day — swimming, running, or tennis — and they settle on tennis.
Source: Lesson 6 – 週末去打網球吧！ (Dialogue 1), mode: book
7 turns — one per line of the book's dialogue, exactly as the book has them.

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's Dialogue 1 verbatim from the source packet; pinyin
and English are the book's own from the same packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: Guoan stands with Youmei and Yiwen near the campus sports courts on a sunny afternoon, pointing toward a phone photo of the beach and looking excited]
國安：今天天氣不錯，海邊的風景也很漂亮，我想去游泳，妳們想去嗎？
Jīntiān tiānqì búcuò, hǎibiān de fēngjǐng yě hěn piàoliàng, wǒ xiǎng qù yóuyǒng, nǐmen xiǎng qù ma?
The weather is pretty good today, so is the view at the beach. I want to go swimming. Do you want to go?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei raises both hands in a small no gesture, then turns toward Yiwen with a questioning look]
友美：我不會游泳。宜文，妳會游泳嗎？
Wǒ bú huì yóuyǒng. Yíwén, nǐ huì yóuyǒng ma?
I can not swim. Yiwen, can you swim?

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen smiles modestly, one hand making a small so-so gesture as she answers]
宜文：我會游泳，可是游得不太好。
Wǒ huì yóuyǒng, kěshì yóu de bú tài hǎo.
I can swim, but not very well.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Guoan points toward the nearby running track, shifting the group's attention away from the beach idea]
國安：我們去跑步，怎麼樣？
Wǒmen qù pǎobù, zěnmeyàng?
How about we go running then?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei looks at the track, shakes her head, and gestures to show slow running with a sheepish expression]
友美：我不喜歡跑步，我跑步跑得很慢。
Wǒ bù xǐhuān pǎobù, wǒ pǎobù pǎo de hěn màn.
I do not like to run; I run slowly.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yiwen points to the tennis courts and mimes swinging a racket, suggesting a new plan while Guoan and Youmei listen]
宜文：打網球怎麼樣？中明說妳的網球打得很好，我們一起去打吧。
Dǎ wǎngqiú zěnmeyàng? Zhōngmíng shuō nǐ de wǎngqiú dǎ de hěn hǎo, wǒmen yìqǐ qù dǎ ba.
How about playing tennis? Zhongming said you plays it well. Let's go play it together.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Youmei smiles and nods, holding a tennis racket while the three friends start walking toward the court]
友美：好啊。
Hǎo ā.
Okay.

5  棒球    bàngqiú   N      baseball

14 跑步    pǎobù     Vi     to run
15 跑      pǎo       Vi     to run

Key Phrases
1  我想去 + V，妳們想去嗎？  —  我想去游泳，妳們想去嗎？  —  Suggest an activity you want to do and invite friends to join.
2  我不會 + V。  —  我不會游泳。  —  Say you cannot do a skill-based activity.
3  V 得不太好。  —  可是游得不太好。  —  Admit you can do something, but not very well.
4  我們去 + V，怎麼樣？  —  我們去跑步，怎麼樣？  —  Offer a new plan and ask if it sounds okay.
5  我不喜歡 + V。  —  我不喜歡跑步。  —  Give a simple preference against an activity.
6  一起去 + V + 吧。  —  我們一起去打吧。  —  Suggest doing an activity together with a soft 吧 ending.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: Guoan stands with Youmei and Yiwen near the campus sports courts on a sunny afternoon, pointing toward a phone photo of the beach and looking excited]
國安：今天天氣真不錯，海邊風景也很漂亮，我想先去游泳，妳們要一起去嗎？
Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn búcuò, hǎibiān fēngjǐng yě hěn piàoliàng, wǒ xiǎng xiān qù yóuyǒng, nǐmen yào yìqǐ qù ma?
The weather is really nice today, and the beach scenery is beautiful too. I want to go swimming first; do you want to go together?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei raises both hands in a small no gesture, then turns toward Yiwen with a questioning look]
友美：我不太會游泳，所以有一點怕。宜文，妳會不會游泳？
Wǒ bú tài huì yóuyǒng, suǒyǐ yǒu yìdiǎn pà. Yíwén, nǐ huì bú huì yóuyǒng?
I am not very good at swimming, so I am a little scared. Yiwen, can you swim?

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen smiles modestly, one hand making a small so-so gesture as she answers]
宜文：我會游泳，可是只會一點，游得還不太好。
Wǒ huì yóuyǒng, kěshì zhǐ huì yìdiǎn, yóu de hái bú tài hǎo.
I can swim, but only a little, and I still do not swim very well.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Guoan points toward the nearby running track, shifting the group's attention away from the beach idea]
國安：那我們改去跑步，好不好？
Nà wǒmen gǎi qù pǎobù, hǎo bù hǎo?
Then let's change and go running, okay?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei looks at the track, shakes her head, and gestures to show slow running with a sheepish expression]
友美：我不太喜歡跑步，因為我跑得很慢，也很容易累。
Wǒ bú tài xǐhuān pǎobù, yīnwèi wǒ pǎo de hěn màn, yě hěn róngyì lèi.
I do not really like running, because I run very slowly and get tired easily too.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yiwen points to the tennis courts and mimes swinging a racket, suggesting a new plan while Guoan and Youmei listen]
宜文：打網球怎麼樣？中明說妳網球打得很好，我們一起去打比較好玩。
Dǎ wǎngqiú zěnmeyàng? Zhōngmíng shuō nǐ wǎngqiú dǎ de hěn hǎo, wǒmen yìqǐ qù dǎ bǐjiào hǎowán.
How about playing tennis? Zhongming said you play tennis well. It will be more fun if we go play together.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Youmei smiles and nods, holding a tennis racket while the three friends start walking toward the court]
友美：好啊，那我們一起去打網球吧。
Hǎo ā, nà wǒmen yìqǐ qù dǎ wǎngqiú ba.
Okay, then let's go play tennis together.

Vocabulary
1  真      zhēn       Adv   really
2  先      xiān       Adv   first
3  一起    yìqǐ       Adv   together
4  不太    bú tài     Adv   not very; not too
5  所以    suǒyǐ      Conj  so; therefore
6  有一點  yǒu yìdiǎn Adv   a little
7  怕      pà         Vs    to be afraid
8  只      zhǐ        Adv   only
9  改      gǎi        V     to change; to switch
10 因為    yīnwèi     Conj  because
11 容易    róngyì     Vs    easy; likely to
12 累      lèi        Vs    tired
13 比較    bǐjiào     Adv   comparatively; more
14 好玩    hǎowán     Vs    fun

Key Phrases
1  真 + Adj  —  今天天氣真不錯。  —  Add a natural spoken boost to an adjective.
2  不太 + V/Adj  —  我不太會游泳。  —  Soften a negative statement without making it absolute.
3  所以…  —  所以有一點怕。  —  Give a simple result after explaining a reason or situation.
4  那我們改去 + V，好不好？  —  那我們改去跑步，好不好？  —  Propose switching plans and invite agreement.
5  因為…也…  —  因為我跑得很慢，也很容易累。  —  Give one reason and add a second related detail.
6  比較 + Adj  —  我們一起去打比較好玩。  —  Compare options in a soft, conversational way.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: Guoan stands with Youmei and Yiwen near the campus sports courts on a sunny afternoon, pointing toward a phone photo of the beach and looking excited]
國安：今天天氣這麼好，海邊風景又漂亮，我本來想去游泳，妳們願意一起去嗎？
Jīntiān tiānqì zhème hǎo, hǎibiān fēngjǐng yòu piàoliàng, wǒ běnlái xiǎng qù yóuyǒng, nǐmen yuànyì yìqǐ qù ma?
The weather is so nice today, and the beach scenery is beautiful too. I was thinking of going swimming; would you be willing to go together?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei raises both hands in a small no gesture, then turns toward Yiwen with a questioning look]
友美：我其實不會游泳，去海邊有點不安心。宜文，妳游泳游得怎麼樣？
Wǒ qíshí bú huì yóuyǒng, qù hǎibiān yǒudiǎn bù ānxīn. Yíwén, nǐ yóuyǒng yóu de zěnmeyàng?
Actually I cannot swim, so going to the beach makes me a bit uneasy. Yiwen, how well do you swim?

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen smiles modestly, one hand making a small so-so gesture as she answers]
宜文：我會游，可是游得沒有國安好，只能慢慢游。
Wǒ huì yóu, kěshì yóu de méiyǒu Guó'ān hǎo, zhǐ néng mànmàn yóu.
I can swim, but I do not swim as well as Guoan; I can only swim slowly.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Guoan points toward the nearby running track, shifting the group's attention away from the beach idea]
國安：如果不想游泳，我們改去跑步怎麼樣？
Rúguǒ bù xiǎng yóuyǒng, wǒmen gǎi qù pǎobù zěnmeyàng?
If you do not want to swim, how about we change and go running?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei looks at the track, shakes her head, and gestures to show slow running with a sheepish expression]
友美：跑步比游泳還不適合我，我跑得很慢，一下子就累了。
Pǎobù bǐ yóuyǒng hái bú shìhé wǒ, wǒ pǎo de hěn màn, yíxiàzi jiù lèi le.
Running suits me even less than swimming. I run very slowly and get tired right away.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yiwen points to the tennis courts and mimes swinging a racket, suggesting a new plan while Guoan and Youmei listen]
宜文：不然我們去打網球吧。中明說妳打得很好，大家一起打一定更有意思。
Bùrán wǒmen qù dǎ wǎngqiú ba. Zhōngmíng shuō nǐ dǎ de hěn hǎo, dàjiā yìqǐ dǎ yídìng gèng yǒu yìsi.
Otherwise, let's go play tennis. Zhongming said you play well; if everyone plays together, it will definitely be more interesting.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Youmei smiles and nods, holding a tennis racket while the three friends start walking toward the court]
友美：好啊，網球比跑步好玩，我們現在就去吧。
Hǎo ā, wǎngqiú bǐ pǎobù hǎowán, wǒmen xiànzài jiù qù ba.
Okay. Tennis is more fun than running; let's go now.

Vocabulary
1  這麼    zhème      Adv   so; this much
2  又      yòu        Adv   also; in addition
3  本來    běnlái     Adv   originally; at first
4  願意    yuànyì     Vaux  to be willing to
5  其實    qíshí      Adv   actually
6  不安心  bù ānxīn   Vs    uneasy; not at ease
7  如果    rúguǒ      Conj  if
8  比      bǐ         Prep  compared to; than
9  適合    shìhé      Vst   to suit; to be suitable for
10 一下子  yíxiàzi    Adv   in a short while; all at once
11 不然    bùrán      Conj  otherwise; how about instead
12 大家    dàjiā      N     everyone
13 一定    yídìng     Adv   definitely
14 更      gèng       Adv   even more
15 有意思  yǒu yìsi   Vs    interesting
16 現在就  xiànzài jiù Adv  right now

Key Phrases
1  這麼 + Adj  —  今天天氣這麼好。  —  Emphasize the quality of a situation before suggesting an activity.
2  又 + Adj  —  海邊風景又漂亮。  —  Add a second positive reason after the first one.
3  其實…  —  我其實不會游泳。  —  Correct or clarify something gently before giving your real feeling.
4  如果…，…怎麼樣？  —  如果不想游泳，我們改去跑步怎麼樣？  —  Build a new suggestion around someone else's concern.
5  A 比 B 還不適合我。  —  跑步比游泳還不適合我。  —  Compare two bad options and say one fits you even less.
6  不然我們去 + V + 吧。  —  不然我們去打網球吧。  —  Offer a practical alternative after two plans have failed.
