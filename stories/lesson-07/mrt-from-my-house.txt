《從我家坐捷運很方便》It Is Convenient to Take MRT from My Home
Situation: A student describes the MRT lines near home, everyday trips to school and weekend activities, friends visiting by MRT, and easy airport outings to see scenery and planes.
Source: Lesson 7 – 怎麼到飯店去？ (短文 Reading, track 07-3), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's 短文《從我家坐捷運很方便》verbatim; pinyin and English
are the book's own, from the source packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: outside the narrator's apartment building near an MRT entrance, with a current route map showing red, green, and blue lines beside a small faded old map with fewer lines]
Narrator：我家附近有三條捷運線，一條紅的、一條綠的和一條藍的。
Wǒ jiā fùjìn yǒu sān tiáo jiéyùn xiàn, yì tiáo hóng de, yì tiáo lǜ de hé yì tiáo lán de.
There are three MRT routes near my place; the red one, the green one and the blue one.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the narrator rides the red MRT line with a school backpack, with a gym bag and movie ticket tucked beside the seat for weekend plans]
Narrator：我平常坐紅線去學校上課，週末也坐捷運去運動、看電影。
Wǒ píngcháng zuò hóng xiàn qù xuéxiào shàngkè, zhōumò yě zuò jiéyùn qù yùndòng, kàn diànyǐng.
I usually go to school by the red one. On weekends I take it to exercise or see a movie as well.

[Turn 3 — scene note: friends arrive from the MRT exit and meet the narrator near the apartment, with slow car traffic farther down the street]
Narrator：朋友常來找我，他們都覺得坐捷運來我家，又快又方便。
Péngyǒu cháng lái zhǎo wǒ, tāmen dōu juéde zuò jiéyùn lái wǒ jiā, yòu kuài yòu fāngbiàn.
My friends often visit me. They all think taking the MRT to my house is both fast and convenient.

[Turn 4 — scene note: a road and an MRT track both lead toward an airport terminal, showing a car and an MRT train as two possible ways to go]
Narrator：從我家到機場也不遠，開車去、坐捷運去都可以。
Cóng wǒ jiā dào jīchǎng yě bù yuǎn, kāichē qù, zuò jiéyùn qù dōu kěyǐ.
It is not far from my house to the airport. I can go by car or MRT.

[Turn 5 — scene note: the narrator stands near the airport observation area, looking at open scenery and a plane taking off]
Narrator：機場附近的風景也很漂亮，我常常去看風景、看飛機。
Jīchǎng fùjìn de fēngjǐng yě hěn piàoliàng, wǒ chángcháng qù kàn fēngjǐng, kàn fēijī.
The scenery near the airport is beautiful; I often go there to see the view and watch the planes.

Vocabulary
1  線          xiàn                 N     line (M:條)
2  藍/藍色     lán/lánsè            Vs/N  blue / blue color
3  機場/飛機場  jīchǎng/fēijīchǎng  N     airport
4  開車        kāichē               V-sep to drive a car
5  司機        sījī                 N     driver
6  車站        chēzhàn              N     bus (or rail) station
7  站          zhàn                 N     station
8  飛機        fēijī                N     airplane; aircraft (M:架)
9  飛          fēi                  Vi    to fly
10 起飛        qǐfēi                Vp    to take off (an airplane)

Key Phrases
1  [Place] 附近有 [things]。  —  我家附近有三條捷運線。  —  Say what exists near a place; the location comes first.
2  一條 [color] 的  —  一條紅的、一條綠的和一條藍的。  —  Use 的 after a color when the noun is already understood.
3  坐 [transport] 去 [place/activity]。  —  我平常坐紅線去學校上課。  —  Name the transportation first, then the destination or purpose.
4  又 Adj 又 Adj。  —  又快又方便。  —  Pair two positive descriptions in a compact spoken pattern.
5  從 A 到 B  —  從我家到機場也不遠。  —  Mark a route from one place to another.

[Turn 1 — scene note: outside the narrator's apartment building near an MRT entrance, with a current route map showing red, green, and blue lines beside a small faded old map with fewer lines]
Narrator：我家附近有三條不同的捷運線，有紅線、綠線和藍線。
Wǒ jiā fùjìn yǒu sān tiáo bùtóng de jiéyùn xiàn, yǒu hóng xiàn, lǜ xiàn hé lán xiàn.
Near my house there are three different MRT lines: a red line, a green line, and a blue line.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the narrator rides the red MRT line with a school backpack, with a gym bag and movie ticket tucked beside the seat for weekend plans]
Narrator：我平常坐紅線去學校上課，週末也常坐捷運去運動、看電影。
Wǒ píngcháng zuò hóng xiàn qù xuéxiào shàngkè, zhōumò yě cháng zuò jiéyùn qù yùndòng, kàn diànyǐng.
I usually take the red line to school for class, and on weekends I often take the MRT to exercise or watch a movie.

[Turn 3 — scene note: friends arrive from the MRT exit and meet the narrator near the apartment, with slow car traffic farther down the street]
Narrator：我的朋友下課以後常常來找我，他們都覺得坐捷運來我家很快，也很方便。
Wǒ de péngyǒu xiàkè yǐhòu chángcháng lái zhǎo wǒ, tāmen dōu juéde zuò jiéyùn lái wǒ jiā hěn kuài, yě hěn fāngbiàn.
My friends often come to see me after class. They all think taking the MRT to my house is fast and also convenient.

[Turn 4 — scene note: a road and an MRT track both lead toward an airport terminal, showing a car and an MRT train as two possible ways to go]
Narrator：從我家到機場不太遠，所以開車去、坐捷運去都可以。
Cóng wǒ jiā dào jīchǎng bú tài yuǎn, suǒyǐ kāichē qù, zuò jiéyùn qù dōu kěyǐ.
It is not too far from my house to the airport, so going by car or by MRT both work.

[Turn 5 — scene note: the narrator stands near the airport observation area, looking at open scenery and a plane taking off]
Narrator：機場附近的風景非常漂亮，我週末常常去看風景，也去看飛機。
Jīchǎng fùjìn de fēngjǐng fēicháng piàoliàng, wǒ zhōumò chángcháng qù kàn fēngjǐng, yě qù kàn fēijī.
The scenery near the airport is very beautiful. On weekends I often go to see the scenery and also watch the planes.

Vocabulary
1  不同      bùtóng      Vs    different
2  常        cháng       Adv   often
3  下課      xiàkè       VO    to finish class
4  以後      yǐhòu       N     after; later
5  不太      bú tài      Adv   not too; not very
6  所以      suǒyǐ       Conj  so; therefore
7  非常      fēicháng    Adv   very; extremely
8  週末      zhōumò      N     weekend

Key Phrases
1  三條不同的 [noun]  —  我家附近有三條不同的捷運線。  —  Count several kinds of the same thing and stress they are not the same.
2  週末也常 + V  —  週末也常坐捷運去運動、看電影。  —  Extend a weekday habit into a weekend habit.
3  [time] 以後常常...  —  我的朋友下課以後常常來找我。  —  Put a simple time frame before a repeated action.
4  因果: ..., 所以...  —  從我家到機場不太遠，所以開車去、坐捷運去都可以。  —  Give a reason first, then the practical result.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: outside the narrator's apartment building near an MRT entrance, with a current route map showing red, green, and blue lines beside a small faded old map with fewer lines]
Narrator：我家附近的捷運線比以前多了，有紅線、綠線和藍線，去哪裡都方便。
Wǒ jiā fùjìn de jiéyùn xiàn bǐ yǐqián duō le, yǒu hóng xiàn, lǜ xiàn hé lán xiàn, qù nǎlǐ dōu fāngbiàn.
There are more MRT lines near my house than before: red, green, and blue, so going anywhere is convenient.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the narrator rides the red MRT line with a school backpack, with a gym bag and movie ticket tucked beside the seat for weekend plans]
Narrator：我平常都坐紅線去學校，如果週末有空，也會坐捷運去運動、看電影。
Wǒ píngcháng dōu zuò hóng xiàn qù xuéxiào, rúguǒ zhōumò yǒu kòng, yě huì zuò jiéyùn qù yùndòng, kàn diànyǐng.
I usually take the red line to school. If I have free time on the weekend, I also take the MRT to exercise or see a movie.

[Turn 3 — scene note: friends arrive from the MRT exit and meet the narrator near the apartment, with slow car traffic farther down the street]
Narrator：朋友來過我家以後，都覺得坐捷運比開車快，也方便多了。
Péngyǒu lái guò wǒ jiā yǐhòu, dōu juéde zuò jiéyùn bǐ kāichē kuài, yě fāngbiàn duō le.
After my friends have come to my house, they all feel that taking the MRT is faster than driving, and much more convenient too.

[Turn 4 — scene note: a road and an MRT track both lead toward an airport terminal, showing a car and an MRT train as two possible ways to go]
Narrator：從我家到機場比到市中心近一點，開車或坐捷運去都沒問題。
Cóng wǒ jiā dào jīchǎng bǐ dào shì zhōngxīn jìn yìdiǎn, kāichē huò zuò jiéyùn qù dōu méi wèntí.
From my house, the airport is a little closer than downtown, and going by car or MRT is both no problem.

[Turn 5 — scene note: the narrator stands near the airport observation area, looking at open scenery and a plane taking off]
Narrator：我去過機場附近很多次，覺得那裡的風景最漂亮，飛機起飛也很好看。
Wǒ qù guò jīchǎng fùjìn hěn duō cì, juéde nàlǐ de fēngjǐng zuì piàoliàng, fēijī qǐfēi yě hěn hǎokàn.
I have been near the airport many times, and I think the scenery there is the prettiest; planes taking off are also great to watch.

Vocabulary
1  以前      yǐqián       N     before; in the past
2  哪裡      nǎlǐ         QW    where
3  如果      rúguǒ        Conj  if
4  有空      yǒu kòng     Vs    to have free time
5  會        huì          Aux   will; be likely to
6  過        guò          Ptc   experiential marker
7  市中心    shì zhōngxīn N     city center; downtown
8  或        huò          Conj  or
9  沒問題    méi wèntí     phrase no problem
10 次        cì           M     time; occurrence
11 那裡      nàlǐ         N     there
12 好看      hǎokàn       Vs    good to watch; nice-looking

Key Phrases
1  A 比以前 Adj 了。  —  我家附近的捷運線比以前多了。  —  Compare the present with the past and mark a change.
2  如果...，也會...  —  如果週末有空，也會坐捷運去運動、看電影。  —  Give a simple condition and say what you usually do then.
3  V 過...以後  —  朋友來過我家以後。  —  Talk about what people think or do after having an experience.
4  A 比 B + Adj  —  坐捷運比開車快。  —  Make a direct comparison between two options.
5  覺得...最...  —  覺得那裡的風景最漂亮。  —  Mark an opinion, then say what ranks highest for you.
