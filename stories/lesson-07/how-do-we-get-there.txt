《怎麼到飯店去？》How Do We Get to the Hotel?
Situation: Youmei and Yuanzhen plan how to get to Tianmei Hotel tomorrow to meet Yuanzhen's Korean friend, but class starts before they finish checking the route.
Source: Lesson 7 – 怎麼到飯店去？ (Dialogue 1), mode: book
8 turns — one per line of the book's dialogue, exactly as the book has them.

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's Dialogue 1 verbatim from the source packet; pinyin
and English are the book's own companion lines.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: During break time at school, Youmei stands beside Yuanzhen's desk with a small notebook open, asking about tomorrow's plan]
友美：元真，我們明天幾點去找妳的韓國朋友？
Yuánzhēn, wǒmen míngtiān jǐ diǎn qù zhǎo nǐ de Hánguó péngyǒu?
Yuanzhen, what time are we going to meet up with your Korean friends tomorrow?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen points to the time on her phone, with a simple hotel icon and route line visible on the screen]
元真：我們下午四點要到天美飯店，她在那裡等我們。
Wǒmen xiàwǔ sì diǎn yào dào Tiānměi Fàndiàn, tā zài nàlǐ děng wǒmen.
We have to arrive at Tianmei Hotel at 4 in the afternoon. She will wait for us there.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Youmei holds her transit card and gestures toward a window where a bus and MRT train are visible outside]
友美：我們要怎麼去？坐捷運還是坐公車？
Wǒmen yào zěnme qù? Zuò jiéyùn háishì zuò gōngchē?
How do we get there? By MRT or bus?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks toward the school gate and points down the nearby street, confident the hotel is close enough to walk]
元真：聽說那家飯店不遠，我想我們可以從學校走路去。
Tīngshuō nà jiā fàndiàn bù yuǎn, wǒ xiǎng wǒmen kěyǐ cóng xuéxiào zǒulù qù.
I heard that the hotel is not far; I think we can go there from the school on foot.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei frowns at Yuanzhen's phone map, tracing the route with one finger but still looking unsure]
友美：可是我不知道怎麼走，妳知道嗎？
Kěshì wǒ bù zhīdào zěnme zǒu, nǐ zhīdào ma?
But I don't know how to get there, do you?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen raises her phone between them and opens an online map while both girls lean in to look]
元真：我也不知道。我們可以先上網看地圖。
Wǒ yě bù zhīdào. Wǒmen kěyǐ xiān shàngwǎng kàn dìtú.
I don't know either. We can check the map online first.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Looking at her watch, Youmei suddenly notices class is starting and grabs her textbook from the desk]
友美：啊！上課了！我們先上課吧。
Ā! Shàngkè le! Wǒmen xiān shàngkè ba.
Ah! The class has started! Let's go to class now.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods, closes the map on her phone, and follows Youmei back toward the classroom door]
元真：好。
Hǎo.
Okay.

Vocabulary
1  怎麼        zěnme          Adv    how
2  到          dào            Prep   to
3  飯店        fàndiàn        N      hotel (M: 家)
4  旅館        lǚguǎn         N      inn; hotel (M: 家)
5  找          zhǎo           V      to visit; to see (a person)
6  韓國        Hánguó         N      Korea
7  到          dào            V      to arrive (a place)
8  等          děng           V      to wait for
9  坐          zuò            V      to take (transportation)
10 捷運/地鐵   jiéyùn/dìtiě   N      MRT; metro; subway
11 公車        gōngchē        N      bus (M: 部/輛)
12 遠          yuǎn           Vs     far
13 近          jìn            Vs     near
14 從          cóng           Prep   from
15 走路        zǒulù          V-sep  to walk (on foot)
16 走          zǒu            Vi     to walk (on foot)
17 先          xiān           Adv    first; in advance
18 地圖        dìtú           N      map (M: 張)
19 錶/手錶     biǎo/shǒubiǎo N      watch (M: 個)
20 了          le             Ptc    particle indicating changed situations

Key Phrases
1  幾點 + V？ — 我們明天幾點去找妳的韓國朋友？ — Ask what time a planned action will happen.
2  [time] 要到 [place] — 我們下午四點要到天美飯店，她在那裡等我們。 — State a required arrival time and place.
3  坐 A 還是坐 B？ — 坐捷運還是坐公車？ — Offer two transportation choices.
4  聽說...，我想... — 聽說那家飯店不遠，我想我們可以從學校走路去。 — Report what you heard, then give your own plan.
5  先 + V — 我們可以先上網看地圖。 — Say what to do first before deciding the next step.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)

[Turn 1 — scene note: During break time at school, Youmei stands beside Yuanzhen's desk with a small notebook open, asking about tomorrow's plan]
友美：元真，我們明天下午幾點去找妳的韓國朋友？
Yuánzhēn, wǒmen míngtiān xiàwǔ jǐ diǎn qù zhǎo nǐ de Hánguó péngyǒu?
Yuanzhen, what time tomorrow afternoon are we going to meet up with your Korean friend?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen points to the time on her phone, with a simple hotel icon and route line visible on the screen]
元真：我們下午四點以前要到天美飯店，她會在那裡等我們。
Wǒmen xiàwǔ sì diǎn yǐqián yào dào Tiānměi Fàndiàn, tā huì zài nàlǐ děng wǒmen.
We need to arrive at Tianmei Hotel before four in the afternoon. She will be waiting for us there.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Youmei holds her transit card and gestures toward a window where a bus and MRT train are visible outside]
友美：我們應該怎麼去？坐捷運比較快，還是坐公車比較方便？
Wǒmen yīnggāi zěnme qù? Zuò jiéyùn bǐjiào kuài, háishì zuò gōngchē bǐjiào fāngbiàn?
How should we go? Is taking the MRT faster, or is taking the bus more convenient?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks toward the school gate and points down the nearby street, confident the hotel is close enough to walk]
元真：聽說那家飯店離學校不太遠，我覺得我們可以直接走路去。
Tīngshuō nà jiā fàndiàn lí xuéxiào bù tài yuǎn, wǒ juéde wǒmen kěyǐ zhíjiē zǒulù qù.
I heard that hotel is not too far from school. I think we can walk there directly.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei frowns at Yuanzhen's phone map, tracing the route with one finger but still looking unsure]
友美：可是我還不知道怎麼走，妳可以先告訴我嗎？
Kěshì wǒ hái bù zhīdào zěnme zǒu, nǐ kěyǐ xiān gàosu wǒ ma?
But I still don't know how to get there. Can you tell me first?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen raises her phone between them and opens an online map while both girls lean in to look]
元真：我也不太清楚。我們先上網看地圖，再決定怎麼走。
Wǒ yě bù tài qīngchǔ. Wǒmen xiān shàngwǎng kàn dìtú, zài juédìng zěnme zǒu.
I'm not very sure either. Let's check the map online first, then decide how to go.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Looking at her watch, Youmei suddenly notices class is starting and grabs her textbook from the desk]
友美：啊，已經上課了！我們先回教室上課吧。
Ā, yǐjīng shàngkè le! Wǒmen xiān huí jiàoshì shàngkè ba.
Ah, class has already started! Let's go back to the classroom first.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods, closes the map on her phone, and follows Youmei back toward the classroom door]
元真：好，我們下課以後再看地圖。
Hǎo, wǒmen xiàkè yǐhòu zài kàn dìtú.
Okay, we'll look at the map again after class.

3  會          huì         Adv    will; likely to
4  應該        yīnggāi     Adv    should
5  比較        bǐjiào      Adv    comparatively; more
6  方便        fāngbiàn    Vs     convenient
7  離          lí          Prep   from; away from
8  不太        bù tài      Adv    not too; not very
9  覺得        juéde       Vst    to feel; to think
10 直接        zhíjiē      Adv    directly
11 還          hái         Adv    still
12 告訴        gàosu       V      to tell
13 清楚        qīngchǔ     Vs     clear; to be clear
14 再          zài         Adv    then; again
15 決定        juédìng     V      to decide
16 已經        yǐjīng      Adv    already
17 教室        jiàoshì     N      classroom

Key Phrases
1  [time] 以前要到 [place] — 我們下午四點以前要到天美飯店，她會在那裡等我們。 — Use this when a place has an arrival deadline.
2  A 比較 Adj，還是 B 比較 Adj？ — 坐捷運比較快，還是坐公車比較方便？ — Compare two practical choices without making a final decision yet.
3  離 [place] 不太遠 — 聽說那家飯店離學校不太遠，我覺得我們可以直接走路去。 — Describe a place as close enough that walking may work.
4  先 V，再 V — 我們先上網看地圖，再決定怎麼走。 — Put two actions in order: first check, then decide.
5  [event] 以後再 V — 好，我們下課以後再看地圖。 — Postpone a task until after another event.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: During break time at school, Youmei stands beside Yuanzhen's desk with a small notebook open, asking about tomorrow's plan]
友美：元真，我們明天幾點出發，去找妳那位韓國朋友？
Yuánzhēn, wǒmen míngtiān jǐ diǎn chūfā, qù zhǎo nǐ nà wèi Hánguó péngyǒu?
Yuanzhen, what time are we leaving tomorrow to go meet that Korean friend of yours?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen points to the time on her phone, with a simple hotel icon and route line visible on the screen]
元真：我們下午四點前一定要到天美飯店，她會先在大廳等我們。
Wǒmen xiàwǔ sì diǎn qián yídìng yào dào Tiānměi Fàndiàn, tā huì xiān zài dàtīng děng wǒmen.
We definitely need to arrive at Tianmei Hotel before four in the afternoon. She will wait for us in the lobby first.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Youmei holds her transit card and gestures toward a window where a bus and MRT train are visible outside]
友美：那我們怎麼過去？搭捷運比坐公車快，還是公車比較方便？
Nà wǒmen zěnme guòqù? Dā jiéyùn bǐ zuò gōngchē kuài, háishì gōngchē bǐjiào fāngbiàn?
Then how do we get over there? Is taking the MRT faster than taking the bus, or is the bus more convenient?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks toward the school gate and points down the nearby street, confident the hotel is close enough to walk]
元真：聽說那家飯店離學校很近，走路比坐車方便，我們從學校出發就可以。
Tīngshuō nà jiā fàndiàn lí xuéxiào hěn jìn, zǒulù bǐ zuòchē fāngbiàn, wǒmen cóng xuéxiào chūfā jiù kěyǐ.
I heard that hotel is very close to school. Walking is more convenient than riding, so we can just leave from school.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Youmei frowns at Yuanzhen's phone map, tracing the route with one finger but still looking unsure]
友美：可是路線我不熟，如果走錯路怎麼辦？妳知道方向嗎？
Kěshì lùxiàn wǒ bù shú, rúguǒ zǒu cuò lù zěnme bàn? Nǐ zhīdào fāngxiàng ma?
But I'm not familiar with the route. What if we go the wrong way? Do you know the direction?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen raises her phone between them and opens an online map while both girls lean in to look]
元真：我也不熟。我們先上網查地圖，知道路線以後再走。
Wǒ yě bù shú. Wǒmen xiān shàngwǎng chá dìtú, zhīdào lùxiàn yǐhòu zài zǒu.
I'm not familiar with it either. Let's check the map online first and leave after we know the route.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Looking at her watch, Youmei suddenly notices class is starting and grabs her textbook from the desk]
友美：啊，已經上課了！我們先進教室，下課以後再看吧。
Ā, yǐjīng shàngkè le! Wǒmen xiān jìn jiàoshì, xiàkè yǐhòu zài kàn ba.
Ah, class has already started! Let's go into the classroom first and look again after class.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods, closes the map on her phone, and follows Youmei back toward the classroom door]
元真：好，等一下我傳地圖給妳。
Hǎo, děng yíxià wǒ chuán dìtú gěi nǐ.
Okay, I'll send you the map in a little while.

Vocabulary
1  出發        chūfā       V      to set out; to leave
2  那位        nà wèi      Det-M  that person; that one (polite measure)
3  前          qián        N      before
4  一定        yídìng      Adv    definitely; certainly
5  大廳        dàtīng      N      lobby; hall
6  過去        guòqù       V      to go over; to get there
7  搭          dā          V      to take (transportation)
8  比          bǐ          Prep   compared with; than
9  坐車        zuòchē      V-sep  to ride in a vehicle
10 路線        lùxiàn      N      route
11 熟          shú         Vs     familiar
12 如果        rúguǒ       Conj   if
13 走錯路      zǒu cuò lù  VO     to go the wrong way
14 方向        fāngxiàng   N      direction
15 查          chá         V      to check; to look up
16 進          jìn         V      to enter
17 傳          chuán       V      to send; to pass along

Key Phrases
1  [time] 前一定要到 [place] — 我們下午四點前一定要到天美飯店，她會先在大廳等我們。 — Make an arrival time sound firm and important.
2  A 比 B + Adj — 搭捷運比坐公車快，還是公車比較方便？ — Compare two transportation choices directly.
3  走路比坐車 Adj — 聽說那家飯店離學校很近，走路比坐車方便，我們從學校出發就可以。 — Explain why walking beats taking a vehicle for a short trip.
4  如果...怎麼辦？ — 如果走錯路怎麼辦？ — Raise a practical worry before making a plan.
5  V 以後再 V — 我們先上網查地圖，知道路線以後再走。 — Say one action should happen only after a condition is clear.
6  等一下 + V 給妳 — 好，等一下我傳地圖給妳。 — Promise to send or give something after the immediate interruption passes.
