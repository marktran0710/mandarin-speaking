Image prompt for 《我的房間》My Room
4 turns → 2 columns × 2 rows. Turn count follows
the source text — never adjusted to suit a grid.

HOW TO USE THIS FILE — generate ONE image, then edit it twice, all in the
SAME chat:
  1. Paste the IMAGE PROMPT — EASY block. This generates the base picture.
  2. Paste the IMAGE EDIT PROMPT — MEDIUM block right after, in the same
     chat. This asks the model to edit the image it just made — same
     artwork, only the bubble text changes — instead of drawing a new one.
  3. Paste the IMAGE EDIT PROMPT — HARD block the same way, still in the
     same chat (it edits the EASY image again, not the MEDIUM one, so
     changes don't stack).
Editing preserves far more of the actual picture than a fresh generation
does, because the model is transforming an image it can see rather than
reconstructing the scene from text. If your chat doesn't carry the image
forward (a new conversation, a different device), upload the EASY image
file before pasting the edit prompt.

This still will not produce byte-identical artwork across all three — an
edit can nudge a line or a colour. For that, use the local compositing
route in SKILL.md step 12 instead.

(Chinese text in AI-generated images is unreliable — proofread every
caption box against the CAPTION SCRIPT under each prompt and regenerate
if any characters look wrong.)

Single image — one comic page of 4 panels in a STRICT UNIFORM GRID of
2 COLUMNS × 2 ROWS, laid out exactly like this:

+---------+---------+
| Panel 1 | Panel 2 |
+---------+---------+
| Panel 3 | Panel 4 |
+---------+---------+

Every panel is EXACTLY THE SAME SIZE: identical width, identical height, each
a landscape rectangle roughly 4:3, edges aligned so the columns and rows line
up, equal thin light gutters, no hard comic-book borders. No panel may span
two cells, no panel may be larger than another, and a panel's size does not
change because its scene has more detail in it. No splash panel, no inset, no
overlapping frame, no dynamic irregular comic layout.

ONE PANEL = ONE SENTENCE. All 4 sentences listed below must appear, each alone in
its own panel, in reading order.
Every panel has exactly one rectangular caption box along its bottom edge —
plain white with a thin dark border, spanning the panel's width, no tail
(this story is narration, nobody speaks on the page). Never two boxes in a
panel, never a panel without one.

Make the bubbles and boxes generously sized — roughly the top third of the
panel, with the characters composed into the lower two thirds (this story's
HARD lines are the longest — leave room for them even though this is the EASY
image, since the next two prompts reuse these same bubble positions). No other
text anywhere in the image: no title, no level label, no panel numbers, no
signage, no letters of any alphabet outside the bubbles and boxes.

COUNT CHECK — before finishing, count the panels: exactly 4 panels, filling
the 2×2 grid. If a sentence has nowhere to go, a panel has been dropped: add
it back rather than merging two sentences into one bubble. Panel 4 is the ending
of the story — a page without it is not the story.

Flat digital illustration style, soft rounded shapes, warm late-afternoon
colour palette (cream walls, honey-coloured wood, muted sage green and soft
orange accents), limited colour palette, gentle clean outlines, friendly
simple style, consistent lighting and colour across every panel. All panels show the same single bedroom from different angles.

CHARACTERS AND FIXED PROPS (identical in every panel they appear in):
[THE STUDENT] a young man, about 19, wavy dark hair falling over his
forehead, slim build, plain forest-green t-shirt and grey shorts, barefoot.
[THE CAT] a small ginger tabby with white paws and a white chest.
[THE ROOM] cream walls, honey-coloured wooden floor, one wooden desk, one
neatly made single bed with a navy blanket, one sage-green two-seater sofa,
one tall bookshelf, and one large window with a thin cream curtain — the
same furniture in the same positions in every panel.

PANELS:
Panel 1 (top row, left cell): wide establishing shot of the whole room from
the doorway, slightly high angle, showing the wooden desk against the left
wall, the neatly made bed along the back wall, and the sage-green sofa
facing into the room on the right; no people, warm afternoon light across
the floor.
Panel 2 (top row, right cell): medium shot at eye level from the side, a
modern flat-screen television standing dark and switched off on a low stand
in front of the sofa, and the student curled up sideways on the sofa behind
it with his knees drawn up and an open book in both hands, absorbed in
reading, ignoring the TV completely.
Panel 3 (bottom row, left cell): close-up on the tall bookshelf and the
stack of books on the desk beside it, shot straight-on, crowded rows of
colourful book spines — keep all titles as abstract unreadable marks, not
real words; no people in this panel.
Panel 4 (bottom row, right cell): low wide shot from floor level, the large
window with the cream curtain pushed aside, a bright rectangle of sunlight
falling across the floorboards, and the ginger cat stretched out flat on its
side in the middle of that sunlight with its eyes closed, deeply asleep; no
people.

TEXT FOR EACH PANEL (EASY level):
Panel 1 (caption box): 我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Panel 2 (caption box): 沙發前面有一台很新的電視機，可是我不常看電視，我最喜歡在沙發上看書。
Panel 3 (caption box): 我的書很多，有中文書，也有英文書。
Panel 4 (caption box): 我房間的窗戶很大，我的貓喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。

TEXT ACCURACY: reproduce the Chinese characters exactly as written above. Do
not invent, simplify, or substitute characters, and do not add any text that
is not listed here.

EXPORT: about 4:3 (e.g. 1024 × 768). Export as JPG at 80-85% quality,
keeping backgrounds plain so each panel stays legible when scaled down.

Panel 1 — Narrator：我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
  Wǒ de fángjiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
  — There is a table, a bed, and a sofa in my room.
Panel 2 — Narrator：沙發前面有一台很新的電視機，可是我不常看電視，我最喜歡在沙發上看書。
  Shāfā qiánmiàn yǒu yì tái hěn xīn de diànshìjī, kěshì wǒ bù cháng kàn diànshì, wǒ zuì xǐhuān zài shāfā shàng kànshū.
  — There is a new television in front of the sofa, but I do not watch television often. I like to read on the sofa the most.
Panel 3 — Narrator：我的書很多，有中文書，也有英文書。
  Wǒ de shū hěn duō, yǒu Zhōngwén shū, yě yǒu Yīngwén shū.
  — I have many books. I have books in Chinese and in English as well.
Panel 4 — Narrator：我房間的窗戶很大，我的貓喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
  Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù hěn dà, wǒ de māo xǐhuān zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
  — The window in my room is big. My cat likes to bask in the sun next to it.
