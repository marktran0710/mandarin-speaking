《週末去喝下午茶吧！》Let's Go Have Afternoon Tea This Weekend!
Situation: Zhongming invites Youmei to afternoon tea at a café known for ice cream after she says she plans to stay home on the weekend, and they agree on Saturday at 2:30 p.m.
Source: Lesson 5 – 我的錢包在哪裡？Where Is My Wallet? (Dialogue 1), mode: book
9 turns — one per line of the book's dialogue, exactly as the book has them.

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's Dialogue 1 verbatim from the source packet; pinyin
and English are the book's own from the same packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: Zhongming walks up to Youmei's desk in the classroom after class, raising one hand in a friendly greeting as she turns toward him]
中明：友美，妳這個週末要做什麼？
Zhōngmíng: Yǒuměi, nǐ zhè ge zhōumò yào zuò shénme?
Zhongming: Youmei, what are you going to do this weekend?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei sits at her desk with a book, earbuds, and a small stack of notebooks nearby, looking slightly tired but relieved as she describes her quiet weekend plan]
友美：我要在家看書、聽音樂。
Wǒ yào zài jiā kànshū, tīng yīnyuè.
I am going to read and listen to music at home.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Zhongming gestures invitingly, with a simple Saturday afternoon calendar card and tea cup in the visual space between them]
中明：妳星期六下午有空嗎？我們一起去喝下午茶，怎麼樣？
Nǐ xīngqí liù xiàwǔ yǒukòng ma? Wǒmen yìqǐ qù hē xiàwǔ chá, zěnmeyàng?
Are you available on Saturday afternoon? How about we have afternoon tea together?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Youmei smiles brightly and leans forward with curiosity, with a wordless thought bubble showing a tea cup and flipped calendar pages]
友美：好啊！你想去哪裡？
Hǎo ā! Nǐ xiǎng qù nǎlǐ?
Sounds great! Where do you want to go?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Zhongming proudly recommends a café, with a wordless thought bubble showing him previously at a café table and one highlighted ice cream beside dimmer bowls]
中明：我知道一家很不錯的咖啡廳，那裡的冰淇淋很好吃。
Wǒ zhīdào yì jiā hěn búcuò de kāfēitīng, nàlǐ de bīngqílín hěn hǎochī.
I know a nice café. They serve delicious ice cream.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Youmei's eyes light up as she imagines a scoop of chocolate ice cream]
友美：他們有巧克力冰淇淋嗎？
Tāmen yǒu qiǎokèlì bīngqílín ma?
Do they have chocolate ice cream?

[Turn 7 — scene note: Zhongming grins and spreads both hands wide, suggesting many colorful ice cream choices with the chocolate scoop largest and frontmost]
中明：有啊！他們有很多種冰淇淋。
Yǒu ā! Tāmen yǒu hěn duō zhǒng bīngqílín.
Oh, yeah. They have lots of kinds of ice cream.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Youmei stands beside her desk and points toward a simple wall clock set to half past two]
友美：我們這個星期六下午兩點半去吧。
Wǒmen zhè ge xīngqí liù xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn qù ba.
Let's go this Saturday at 2:30 p.m.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Zhongming and Youmei nod at each other with satisfied smiles, the plan settled]
中明：好。
Hǎo.
Okay.

Vocabulary
1  錢包      qiánbāo        N         wallet; purse
2  在        zài            Vst/Prep  (to be located) at; in; on
3  哪裡/哪兒  nǎlǐ/nǎr       N         where
4  聽        tīng           V         to listen
5  音樂      yīnyuè         N         music
6  有空      yǒukòng        Vs        free; at leisure; available
7  下午茶    xiàwǔ chá      N         afternoon tea
8  不錯      búcuò          Vs        not bad
9  咖啡廳    kāfēitīng      N         café
10 那裡/那兒  nàlǐ/nàr       N         there
11 這裡/這兒  zhèlǐ/zhèr     N         here
12 冰淇淋    bīngqílín      N         ice cream
13 巧克力    qiǎokèlì       N         chocolate
14 半        bàn            N         half
15 吧        ba             Ptc       final particle for suggestion

Key Phrases
1  這個週末要做什麼？  —  妳這個週末要做什麼？  —  Ask someone about their plans for the coming weekend.
2  在 + place + V  —  我要在家看書、聽音樂。  —  Say where an activity will happen before naming the activity.
3  X 有空嗎？  —  妳星期六下午有空嗎？  —  Ask if someone is free before making an invitation.
4  我們一起去 + V，怎麼樣？  —  我們一起去喝下午茶，怎麼樣？  —  Suggest doing something together and ask how it sounds.
5  有...嗎？  —  他們有巧克力冰淇淋嗎？  —  Ask whether a place has or serves something.
6  ...吧。  —  我們這個星期六下午兩點半去吧。  —  Add 吧 to make a soft "let's..." suggestion.

[Turn 1 — scene note: Zhongming walks up to Youmei's desk in the classroom after class, raising one hand in a friendly greeting as she turns toward him]
中明：友美，週末快到了，妳這個週末想做什麼？
Yǒuměi, zhōumò kuài dào le, nǐ zhè ge zhōumò xiǎng zuò shénme?
Youmei, the weekend is almost here. What do you want to do this weekend?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei sits at her desk with a book, earbuds, and a small stack of notebooks nearby, looking slightly tired but relieved as she describes her quiet weekend plan]
友美：我想在家看書、聽音樂，也好好休息一下。
Wǒ xiǎng zài jiā kànshū, tīng yīnyuè, yě hǎohǎo xiūxí yíxià.
I want to read and listen to music at home, and also get a good rest.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Zhongming gestures invitingly, with a simple Saturday afternoon calendar card and tea cup in the visual space between them]
中明：妳星期六下午有空嗎？我們一起去咖啡廳喝下午茶，怎麼樣？
Nǐ xīngqí liù xiàwǔ yǒukòng ma? Wǒmen yìqǐ qù kāfēitīng hē xiàwǔ chá, zěnmeyàng?
Are you free Saturday afternoon? How about we go to a café for afternoon tea together?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Youmei smiles brightly and leans forward with curiosity, with a wordless thought bubble showing a tea cup and flipped calendar pages]
友美：好啊！可是你想去哪裡？
Hǎo ā! Kěshì nǐ xiǎng qù nǎlǐ?
Sure! But where do you want to go?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Zhongming proudly recommends a café, with a wordless thought bubble showing him previously at a café table and one highlighted ice cream beside dimmer bowls]
中明：我知道一家很不錯的咖啡廳，那裡的冰淇淋特別好吃。
Wǒ zhīdào yì jiā hěn búcuò de kāfēitīng, nàlǐ de bīngqílín tèbié hǎochī.
I know a really nice café. The ice cream there is especially delicious.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Youmei's eyes light up as she imagines a scoop of chocolate ice cream]
友美：真的嗎？他們有沒有巧克力冰淇淋？
Zhēnde ma? Tāmen yǒu méi yǒu qiǎokèlì bīngqílín?
Really? Do they have chocolate ice cream?

[Turn 7 — scene note: Zhongming grins and spreads both hands wide, suggesting many colorful ice cream choices with the chocolate scoop largest and frontmost]
中明：有啊！他們有很多種冰淇淋，每一種都很好吃。
Yǒu ā! Tāmen yǒu hěn duō zhǒng bīngqílín, měi yì zhǒng dōu hěn hǎochī.
They do! They have many kinds of ice cream, and every kind is delicious.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Youmei stands beside her desk and points toward a simple wall clock set to half past two]
友美：太好了！那我們這個星期六下午兩點半去吧。
Tài hǎo le! Nà wǒmen zhè ge xīngqí liù xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn qù ba.
Great! Then let's go this Saturday at 2:30 in the afternoon.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Zhongming and Youmei nod at each other with satisfied smiles, the plan settled]
中明：好，星期六下午見！
Hǎo, xīngqí liù xiàwǔ jiàn!
Okay, see you Saturday afternoon!

Vocabulary

2  想          xiǎng          Vaux  to want; would like
3  也          yě             Adv   also; too
4  好好        hǎohǎo         Adv   well; properly
5  休息        xiūxí          V     to rest
6  一下        yíxià          Ph    for a bit; briefly
7  一起        yìqǐ           Adv   together
8  可是        kěshì          Conj  but; however
9  特別        tèbié          Adv   especially
10 真的        zhēnde         Adv   really
11 有沒有      yǒu méi yǒu    Ph    have or not; whether there is

13 太...了    tài...le       Ph    so...!
14 見          jiàn           V     to see; to meet

Key Phrases
1  快...了  —  週末快到了。  —  Say that a time or event is coming soon.
2  也 + VP  —  也好好休息一下。  —  Add one more related plan or detail.
3  可是...  —  好啊！可是你想去哪裡？  —  Agree first, then ask a follow-up question.
4  有沒有 + N？  —  他們有沒有巧克力冰淇淋？  —  Ask a yes/no 有 question in a conversational form.
5  每一種都...  —  每一種都很好吃。  —  Say that something is true for every kind.
6  太...了！  —  太好了！  —  React strongly to good news.

[Turn 1 — scene note: Zhongming walks up to Youmei's desk in the classroom after class, raising one hand in a friendly greeting as she turns toward him]
中明：友美，這個星期快結束了，妳週末打算做什麼？
Yǒuměi, zhè ge xīngqí kuài jiéshù le, nǐ zhōumò dǎsuàn zuò shénme?
Youmei, this week is almost over. What do you plan to do this weekend?

[Turn 2 — scene note: Youmei sits at her desk with a book, earbuds, and a small stack of notebooks nearby, looking slightly tired but relieved as she describes her quiet weekend plan]
友美：這個星期我太忙了，所以週末想在家看書、聽音樂，好好休息一下。
Zhè ge xīngqí wǒ tài máng le, suǒyǐ zhōumò xiǎng zài jiā kànshū, tīng yīnyuè, hǎohǎo xiūxí yíxià.
I have been too busy this week, so this weekend I want to read and listen to music at home, and rest a bit.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Zhongming gestures invitingly, with a simple Saturday afternoon calendar card and tea cup in the visual space between them]
中明：如果妳星期六下午有空，我們一起去喝下午茶，怎麼樣？
Rúguǒ nǐ xīngqí liù xiàwǔ yǒukòng, wǒmen yìqǐ qù hē xiàwǔ chá, zěnmeyàng?
If you are free Saturday afternoon, how about we go have afternoon tea together?

[Turn 4 — scene note: Youmei smiles brightly and leans forward with curiosity, with a wordless thought bubble showing a tea cup and flipped calendar pages]
友美：好啊！我很久沒喝下午茶了，你想去哪裡？
Hǎo ā! Wǒ hěn jiǔ méi hē xiàwǔ chá le, nǐ xiǎng qù nǎlǐ?
Sure! I have not had afternoon tea in a long time. Where do you want to go?

[Turn 5 — scene note: Zhongming proudly recommends a café, with a wordless thought bubble showing him previously at a café table and one highlighted ice cream beside dimmer bowls]
中明：我去過一家很不錯的咖啡廳，那裡的冰淇淋比別家的好吃。
Wǒ qùguò yì jiā hěn búcuò de kāfēitīng, nàlǐ de bīngqílín bǐ biéjiā de hǎochī.
I have been to a really nice café. The ice cream there is tastier than other places'.

[Turn 6 — scene note: Youmei's eyes light up as she imagines a scoop of chocolate ice cream]
友美：真的嗎？那他們有沒有巧克力口味的冰淇淋？
Zhēnde ma? Nà tāmen yǒu méi yǒu qiǎokèlì kǒuwèi de bīngqílín?
Really? Then do they have chocolate-flavored ice cream?

[Turn 7 — scene note: Zhongming grins and spreads both hands wide, suggesting many colorful ice cream choices with the chocolate scoop largest and frontmost]
中明：有啊！他們有很多種冰淇淋，我覺得巧克力的最好吃。
Yǒu ā! Tāmen yǒu hěn duō zhǒng bīngqílín, wǒ juéde qiǎokèlì de zuì hǎochī.
They do! They have many kinds of ice cream, and I think the chocolate one is the best.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Youmei stands beside her desk and points toward a simple wall clock set to half past two]
友美：太好了！那就這個星期六下午兩點半去吧。
Tài hǎo le! Nà jiù zhè ge xīngqí liù xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn qù ba.
Great! Then let's go this Saturday at 2:30 in the afternoon.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Zhongming and Youmei nod at each other with satisfied smiles, the plan settled]
中明：好啊，那星期六下午兩點半見！
Hǎo ā, nà xīngqí liù xiàwǔ liǎng diǎn bàn jiàn!
Sounds good. Then see you Saturday afternoon at 2:30!

Vocabulary
1  結束      jiéshù     V     to end; to finish
2  打算      dǎsuàn     Vaux  to plan; to intend
3  忙        máng       Vs    busy
4  所以      suǒyǐ      Conj  so; therefore
5  如果      rúguǒ      Conj  if
6  很久沒...了 hěn jiǔ méi...le Ph   have not...in a long time
7  過        guò        Ptc   experiential aspect marker
8  比        bǐ         Prep  than; compared with
9  別家      biéjiā     N     other shops; other places
10 覺得      juéde      Vst   to feel; to think
11 最        zuì        Adv   most; the ...-est
12 口味      kǒuwèi     N     flavor; taste
13 那就      nà jiù     Adv   then; in that case

Key Phrases
1  打算 + V  —  妳週末打算做什麼？  —  Ask about someone's plan or intention.
2  所以...  —  所以週末想在家看書、聽音樂。  —  Give a result after explaining the reason.
3  如果...，...怎麼樣？  —  如果妳星期六下午有空，我們一起去喝下午茶，怎麼樣？  —  Make an invitation depend on whether the other person is free.
4  很久沒 + V + 了  —  我很久沒喝下午茶了。  —  Say it has been a long time since doing something.
5  V 過  —  我去過一家很不錯的咖啡廳。  —  Use past experience to support a recommendation.
6  A 比 B + Adj  —  那裡的冰淇淋比別家的好吃。  —  Compare two places or things directly.
7  我覺得...最...  —  我覺得巧克力的最好吃。  —  Give a personal opinion and name the top choice.
8  那就...吧。  —  那就這個星期六下午兩點半去吧。  —  Confirm a decision after hearing enough information.
