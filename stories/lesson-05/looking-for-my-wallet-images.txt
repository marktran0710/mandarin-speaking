Image prompts for 《我的錢包在哪裡？》Where Is My Wallet?
Source: Lesson 5 – 我的錢包在哪裡？ (Dialogue 2), mode: book
7 turns (one per line of the book's dialogue) → 2 columns × 4 rows, with the
final row a single wide panel spanning both columns.

TEXT-FREE VERSION
═══════════════════

Single portrait image, divided into a clean grid of 7 panels: 2 columns × 4
rows, where the bottom row is one wide panel spanning both columns. Thin light
gutters between panels, no hard comic-book borders, no text, no letters, no
words, no speech bubbles, no panel numbers, no captions. Flat digital
illustration style, soft rounded shapes, warm afternoon indoor color palette
(cream walls, light wood floor, muted teal and terracotta accents), limited
color palette, gentle clean outlines, friendly simple style, consistent
lighting and color palette across all panels. All panels take place inside the
same small modern Taiwanese apartment.

CHARACTERS (identical in every panel they appear in):
[ZHONGMING] a young man, about 19, short neat black hair, slim build, round
friendly face, mustard-yellow t-shirt, dark grey jeans, white sneakers, and a
dark green canvas backpack hanging off his right shoulder in every panel.
[MOM] a warm middle-aged woman, short curly dark hair with a few grey strands,
light coral blouse with sleeves rolled to the elbow, navy trousers, and a
striped blue-and-white apron tied at the waist in every panel.

Panel 1 (row 1, left): medium shot at eye level, Zhongming standing in his
bedroom doorway with one hand on the door frame, backpack on one shoulder,
patting his empty trouser pocket with the other hand and calling out to his
left, worried expression; behind him a small desk with books and an unmade
bed.

Panel 2 (row 1, right): wider shot of the living room from a slight side
angle, Mom standing beside a low wooden coffee table, one arm extended
pointing down at a brown leather wallet lying on the table top, calm helpful
expression; a teal sofa and a framed photo on the wall behind her.

Panel 3 (row 2, left): close-up on Zhongming's face, hands and shoulders,
holding the brown wallet up at eye level with both hands and shaking his head,
mouth slightly open, disappointed expression; the blurred edge of the coffee
table in the foreground.

Panel 4 (row 2, right): medium shot from behind Zhongming's shoulder, Mom
half-turned toward the teal sofa, chin tilted toward it and one hand making a
small low gesture down near floor level, questioning expression, eyebrows
raised; the sofa filling the right side of the frame.

Panel 5 (row 3, left): low wide shot from floor level, Zhongming kneeling on
the wooden floor beside the teal sofa, cheek almost touching the ground, right
arm reaching deep underneath the sofa, head turned up and to the side looking
off-panel toward his mom, pleading expression; sofa cushions pushed askew.

Panel 6 (row 3, right): medium shot from slightly behind Zhongming's shoulder,
Mom standing in the kitchen doorway holding a small steaming pot with a cloth,
waving him away with her free hand toward the bedroom, busy but affectionate
expression; a stove and hanging utensils visible behind her.

Panel 7 (row 4, wide panel spanning the full width): close medium shot at desk
height, Zhongming back at his bedroom desk lifting a thick textbook with one
hand to reveal a red wallet underneath, other hand reaching for it, face lit up
with relief and a big smile; the empty doorway he came through visible at the
far left of the wide frame.

CONSISTENCY TIPS:
- Keep the exact same character design (Zhongming's mustard t-shirt and dark
  green backpack, Mom's striped apron and coral blouse) recognizable in every
  panel.
- Keep the two wallets clearly different: the one on the living room table is
  brown leather and slightly larger (the brother's), the one under the book in
  Panel 7 is red and smaller (Zhongming's).
- Keep one art style and one lighting mood across all 7 panels so the page
  reads as one connected apartment.
- Vary pose, shot framing, and camera angle panel to panel so it reads as seven
  different moments, not one pose repeated seven times.
- If one panel looks off after generation, accept minor variance, or generate
  that one beat as its own image and swap it in during editing if it matters.

EXPORT SETTINGS (to keep the file under 1.5 MB):
- Generate at a resolution that keeps individual panels legible — e.g.
  1024 x 1792 for a 2×4 grid.
- Export as JPG (much smaller than PNG) at about 80-85% quality.
- If the file is still too large, lower JPG quality slightly, reduce overall
  dimensions, or simplify panel backgrounds.
- Use PNG only if you need transparency; if so, expect larger files and
  compress with a tool like TinyPNG to get under 1.5 MB.

CONSISTENCY TIPS:
- Keep the exact same character design (Zhongming's mustard t-shirt and dark
  green backpack, Mom's striped apron and coral blouse) recognizable in every
  panel.
- Keep the two wallets clearly different: the one on the living room table is
  brown leather and noticeably larger (the brother's), the one under the book
  in Panel 7 is red and smaller (Zhongming's) — the size difference is what
  Panel 3's line is about.
- Use Traditional Chinese characters only, no pinyin and no English anywhere
  in the image.
- Vary pose, shot framing, and camera angle panel to panel so it reads as seven
  different moments, not one pose repeated seven times.

EXPORT SETTINGS (to keep the file under 1.5 MB):
- Generate at a resolution that keeps individual panels legible — e.g.
  1024 x 1792 for a 2×4 grid.
- Export as JPG (much smaller than PNG) at about 80-85% quality.
- If the file is still too large, lower JPG quality slightly, reduce overall
  dimensions, or simplify panel backgrounds.
- Use PNG only if you need transparency; if so, expect larger files and
