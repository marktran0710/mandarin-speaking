《我的房間》My Room
Situation: A student describes their room: furniture, a TV they rarely watch, many Chinese and English books, and a cat sunbathing by the big window.
Source: Lesson 5 – 我的錢包在哪裡？ (短文 Reading, track 05-3), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's 短文《我的房間》verbatim; pinyin and English
are the book's own, from the provided Lesson 5 reading packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view from the bedroom doorway — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made single bed, and a small sofa all visible in the same tidy room]
Narrator：我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
There is a table, a bed, and a sofa in my room.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the new television sits dark and unwatched in front of the sofa while the student curls up on the sofa with an open book]
Narrator：沙發前面有一台很新的電視機，可是我不常看電視，我最喜歡在沙發上看書。
Shāfā qiánmiàn yǒu yì tái hěn xīn de diànshìjī, kěshì wǒ bù cháng kàn diànshì, wǒ zuì xǐhuān zài shāfā shàng kànshū.
There is a new television in front of the sofa, but I do not watch television often. I like to read on the sofa the most.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close view of a crowded bookshelf and a stack of well-used books on the desk, with bookmarks and open pages suggesting books the student reads often; some covers suggest Chinese books and some suggest English books but no readable titles]
Narrator：我的書很多，有中文書，也有英文書。
Wǒ de shū hěn duō, yǒu Zhōngwén shū, yě yǒu Yīngwén shū.
I have many books. I have books in Chinese and in English as well.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the large bedroom window fills the wall, a door edge shows its size, and a small cat stretches beside the window in a bright patch of sun]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶很大，我的貓喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù hěn dà, wǒ de māo xǐhuān zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is big. My cat likes to bask in the sun next to it.

Vocabulary
1  床        chuáng        N     bed
2  家具      jiājù         N     furniture
3  電視(機)  diànshì(jī)   N     TV (television)
4  窗戶      chuānghù      N     window
5  門        mén           N     door
6  貓        māo           N     cat
7  狗        gǒu           N     dog
8  鳥        niǎo          N     bird
9  隻        zhī           M     measure word for animals
10 曬太陽    shài tàiyáng  VO    to bask in the sun
11 太陽      tàiyáng       N     the sun

Key Phrases
1  [Place] 裡有 [things]。  —  我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。  —  Put the place first, then use 有 to say what exists there.
2  [Thing] 前面有…  —  沙發前面有一台很新的電視機。  —  Locate one thing in relation to another; swap 前面 for 旁邊, 上面, or 裡面.
3  我最喜歡 + V。  —  我最喜歡在沙發上看書。  —  Say what you like doing most.
4  有 A，也有 B。  —  有中文書，也有英文書。  —  Add a second item of the same kind with 也.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view from the bedroom doorway — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made single bed, and a small sofa all visible in the same tidy room]
Narrator：我的房間不大，可是很舒服，裡面有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān bú dà, kěshì hěn shūfú, lǐmiàn yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
My room is not big, but it is comfortable. Inside there is a table, a bed, and a sofa.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the new television sits dark and unwatched in front of the sofa while the student curls up on the sofa with an open book]
Narrator：沙發前面有一台非常新的電視機，可是我平常不看電視，最喜歡坐在沙發上看書。
Shāfā qiánmiàn yǒu yì tái fēicháng xīn de diànshìjī, kěshì wǒ píngcháng bú kàn diànshì, zuì xǐhuān zuò zài shāfā shàng kànshū.
There is a very new television in front of the sofa, but I usually do not watch TV. I like sitting on the sofa and reading the most.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close view of a crowded bookshelf and a stack of well-used books on the desk, with bookmarks and open pages suggesting books the student reads often; some covers suggest Chinese books and some suggest English books but no readable titles]
Narrator：我的書很多，中文書、英文書我都有，每一本都很好看。
Wǒ de shū hěn duō, Zhōngwén shū, Yīngwén shū wǒ dōu yǒu, měi yì běn dōu hěn hǎokàn.
I have many books. I have both Chinese books and English books, and every one is good to read.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the large bedroom window fills the wall, a door edge shows its size, and a small cat stretches beside the window in a bright patch of sun]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶很大，每天下午我的貓都喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù hěn dà, měitiān xiàwǔ wǒ de māo dōu xǐhuān zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is big. Every afternoon my cat likes to bask in the sun beside the window.

Vocabulary
1  舒服      shūfú       Vs    comfortable
2  裡面      lǐmiàn      N     inside
3  非常      fēicháng    Adv   very; extremely
4  平常      píngcháng   Adv   usually; normally
5  坐        zuò         V     to sit
6  每        měi         Det   every; each
7  本        běn         M     measure word for books
8  好看      hǎokàn      Vs    good to read; nice-looking
9  每天      měitiān     N     every day
10 下午      xiàwǔ       N     afternoon

Key Phrases
1  …不大，可是很…  —  我的房間不大，可是很舒服。  —  Give a small contrast while keeping the tone positive.
2  平常不 + V。  —  我平常不看電視。  —  Describe what you usually do not do.
3  A、B 我都有。  —  中文書、英文書我都有。  —  Put the list first, then use 都有 to include both.
4  每一 + M + 都…  —  每一本都很好看。  —  Say something is true for every single item.
5  每天 + time + 都…  —  每天下午我的貓都喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。  —  Describe a regular daily routine.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view from the bedroom doorway — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made single bed, and a small sofa all visible in the same tidy room]
Narrator：我的房間雖然不大，可是我很喜歡，裡面有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān suīrán bú dà, kěshì wǒ hěn xǐhuān, lǐmiàn yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
Although my room is not big, I like it a lot. Inside there is a table, a bed, and a sofa.

[Turn 2 — scene note: the new television sits dark and unwatched in front of the sofa while the student curls up on the sofa with an open book]
Narrator：沙發前面那台電視機很新，可是我已經很久沒看電視了，我覺得在沙發上看書最舒服。
Shāfā qiánmiàn nà tái diànshìjī hěn xīn, kěshì wǒ yǐjīng hěn jiǔ méi kàn diànshì le, wǒ juéde zài shāfā shàng kànshū zuì shūfú.
That television in front of the sofa is new, but I have not watched TV for a long time. I think reading on the sofa is the most comfortable.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close view of a crowded bookshelf and a stack of well-used books on the desk, with bookmarks and open pages suggesting books the student reads often; some covers suggest Chinese books and some suggest English books but no readable titles]
Narrator：我的書很多，中文書、英文書我都看過，常看的幾本放在桌子上。
Wǒ de shū hěn duō, Zhōngwén shū, Yīngwén shū wǒ dōu kàn guò, cháng kàn de jǐ běn fàng zài zhuōzi shàng.
I have many books. I have read both the Chinese books and the English books, and the few I often read are on the table.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the large bedroom window fills the wall, a door edge shows its size, and a small cat stretches beside the window in a bright patch of sun]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶比房門還大，所以我的貓每天下午都在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù bǐ fángmén hái dà, suǒyǐ wǒ de māo měitiān xiàwǔ dōu zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is even bigger than the bedroom door, so every afternoon my cat basks in the sun beside it.

Vocabulary
1  雖然      suīrán      Conj  although
2  已經      yǐjīng      Adv   already
3  很久沒…了  hěn jiǔ méi…le  Pat  have not done something for a long time
4  覺得      juéde       Vst   to feel; to think
5  過        guò         Ptc   experiential marker; have done before
6  幾        jǐ          Det   a few; several
7  放        fàng        V     to put; to place
8  比        bǐ          Prep  compared with; than
9  房門      fángmén     N     bedroom door
10 還        hái         Adv   even more; still
11 所以      suǒyǐ       Conj  so; therefore

Key Phrases
1  雖然…，可是…  —  我的房間雖然不大，可是我很喜歡。  —  Make a fuller contrast: admit one point, then give the main feeling.
2  已經很久沒 + V + 了。  —  我已經很久沒看電視了。  —  Say you have not done something for a long time.
3  我覺得…最…  —  我覺得在沙發上看書最舒服。  —  Mark an opinion as yours, then rank one option as the best.
4  S + V 過。  —  中文書、英文書我都看過。  —  Say you have done something before; 過 follows the verb.
5  A 比 B 還 + Adj。  —  我房間的窗戶比房門還大。  —  Say A is even more than B, especially when B is already notable.
