《我的房間》My Room
Situation: A student describes his own room — the furniture, the TV he never watches, his books, and the cat that suns itself by the window.
Source: Lesson 5 – 我的錢包在哪裡？ (短文 Reading, track 05-3), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's 短文《我的房間》verbatim, p.95; pinyin and English
are the book's own, from the companion spread on p.96.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view of the whole room — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made bed, and a sofa facing into the room]
Narrator：我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān lǐ yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
There is a table, a bed, and a sofa in my room.

[Turn 2 — scene note: a new flat-screen TV standing dark and unwatched in front of the sofa; the narrator curled up sideways on the sofa with an open book instead]
Narrator：沙發前面有一台很新的電視機，可是我不常看電視，我最喜歡在沙發上看書。
Shāfā qiánmiàn yǒu yì tái hěn xīn de diànshìjī, kěshì wǒ bù cháng kàn diànshì, wǒ zuì xǐhuān zài shāfā shàng kànshū.
There is a new television in front of the sofa, but I do not watch television often. I like to read on the sofa the most.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close on the crowded bookshelf and the stack on the desk — some spines with Chinese titles, some with English titles]
Narrator：我的書很多，有中文書，也有英文書。
Wǒ de shū hěn duō, yǒu Zhōngwén shū, yě yǒu Yīngwén shū.
I have many books. I have books in Chinese and in English as well.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the big bright window with the curtain pushed aside, the cat stretched out flat in the patch of sunlight beside it]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶很大，我的貓喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù hěn dà, wǒ de māo xǐhuān zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is big. My cat likes to bask in the sun next to it.

Vocabulary
1  床        chuáng        N     bed (M:張)
2  台        tái           M     measure word for machines
3  電視(機)  diànshì(jī)   N     TV (television) (M:台)
4  新        xīn           Vs    new
5  常        cháng         Adv   often
6  最        zuì           Adv   most
7  喜歡      xǐhuān        V     to like
8  中文      Zhōngwén      N     Chinese (language)
9  英文      Yīngwén       N     English (language)
10 窗戶      chuānghù      N     window
11 貓        māo           N     cat (M:隻)
12 曬太陽    shài tàiyáng  VO    to bask in the sun
13 前面      qiánmiàn      N     in front of
14 旁邊      pángbiān      N     beside; next to

Key Phrases
1  [Place] 裡有 [things]。  —  我的房間裡有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。  —  The core existential pattern: place first, 有 second, never 是.
2  [Thing] 前面有…  —  沙發前面有一台很新的電視機。  —  Locate one object relative to another; swap in 後面／旁邊／上面 the same way.
3  我最喜歡 + V。  —  我最喜歡在沙發上看書。  —  Name your single favourite activity; 最 sits right before 喜歡.
4  有 A，也有 B。  —  有中文書，也有英文書。  —  List two kinds you have of the same thing; 也 carries the "as well".

MEDIUM (A1-A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view of the whole room — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made bed, and a sofa facing into the room]
Narrator：我的房間不大，可是很舒服，裡面有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān bú dà, kěshì hěn shūfú, lǐmiàn yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
My room isn't big, but it's very comfortable — inside there's a desk, a bed and a sofa.

[Turn 2 — scene note: a new flat-screen TV standing dark and unwatched in front of the sofa; the narrator curled up sideways on the sofa with an open book instead]
Narrator：沙發前面有一台非常新的電視機，可是我平常不看電視，最喜歡坐在沙發上看書。
Shāfā qiánmiàn yǒu yì tái fēicháng xīn de diànshìjī, kěshì wǒ píngcháng bú kàn diànshì, zuì xǐhuān zuò zài shāfā shàng kànshū.
In front of the sofa there's a brand-new television, but I don't usually watch TV — what I like best is sitting on the sofa reading.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close on the crowded bookshelf and the stack on the desk — some spines with Chinese titles, some with English titles]
Narrator：我的書很多，中文書、英文書我都有，每一本都很好看。
Wǒ de shū hěn duō, Zhōngwén shū, Yīngwén shū wǒ dōu yǒu, měi yì běn dōu hěn hǎokàn.
I have a lot of books — Chinese ones and English ones, and every single one is good.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the big bright window with the curtain pushed aside, the cat stretched out flat in the patch of sunlight beside it]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶很大，每天下午我的貓都喜歡在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù hěn dà, měitiān xiàwǔ wǒ de māo dōu xǐhuān zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is big, and every afternoon my cat likes to sun itself beside it.

Vocabulary
1  舒服      shūfú       Vs    comfortable
2  裡面      lǐmiàn      N     inside
3  非常      fēicháng    Adv   very; extremely
4  平常      píngcháng   Adv   usually; normally
5  坐        zuò         V     to sit
6  每        měi         Det   every; each
7  本        běn         M     measure word for books
8  好看      hǎokàn      Vs    good (to read); nice-looking
9  每天      měitiān     N     every day

Key Phrases
1  …不大，可是很…  —  我的房間不大，可是很舒服。  —  Concede a small drawback, then give the real point; the everyday shape of a modest description.
2  A、B 我都有。  —  中文書、英文書我都有。  —  Put the listed items first, then sweep them up with 都.
3  每一 + M + 都…  —  每一本都很好看。  —  Say it's true of every single one; 每 always needs 都 later in the sentence.
4  每天 + [time] + 都…  —  每天下午我的貓都喜歡曬太陽。  —  Describe a daily routine, not a one-off event.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: wide view of the whole room — a wooden desk against one wall, a neatly made bed, and a sofa facing into the room]
Narrator：我的房間雖然不大，可是我很喜歡，裡面有一張桌子、一張床跟一張沙發。
Wǒ de fángjiān suīrán bú dà, kěshì wǒ hěn xǐhuān, lǐmiàn yǒu yì zhāng zhuōzi, yì zhāng chuáng gēn yì zhāng shāfā.
My room may not be big, but I really like it — inside there's a desk, a bed and a sofa.

[Turn 2 — scene note: a new flat-screen TV standing dark and unwatched in front of the sofa; the narrator curled up sideways on the sofa with an open book instead]
Narrator：沙發前面那台電視機比舊的大多了，可是我已經很久沒看電視了，我覺得坐在沙發上看書最舒服。
Shāfā qiánmiàn nà tái diànshìjī bǐ jiù de dà duō le, kěshì wǒ yǐjīng hěn jiǔ méi kàn diànshì le, wǒ juéde zuò zài shāfā shàng kànshū zuì shūfú.
That television in front of the sofa is much bigger than the old one, but I haven't watched TV in ages — to me, sitting on the sofa reading is the most comfortable thing there is.

[Turn 3 — scene note: close on the crowded bookshelf and the stack on the desk — some spines with Chinese titles, some with English titles]
Narrator：我的書比以前多了很多，中文的、英文的我都看過，最喜歡的那幾本就放在桌子上。
Wǒ de shū bǐ yǐqián duō le hěn duō, Zhōngwén de, Yīngwén de wǒ dōu kàn guò, zuì xǐhuān de nà jǐ běn jiù fàng zài zhuōzi shàng.
I have far more books than I used to — I've read both the Chinese and the English ones, and my favourite few sit right on the desk.

[Turn 4 — scene note: the big bright window with the curtain pushed aside, the cat stretched out flat in the patch of sunlight beside it]
Narrator：我房間的窗戶比客廳的還大，所以我的貓每天下午都在窗戶旁邊曬太陽。
Wǒ fángjiān de chuānghù bǐ kètīng de hái dà, suǒyǐ wǒ de māo měitiān xiàwǔ dōu zài chuānghù pángbiān shài tàiyáng.
The window in my room is even bigger than the living room's, so every afternoon my cat suns itself right beside it.

Vocabulary
1  雖然      suīrán     Conj  although
2  舊        jiù        Vs    old (of things)
3  已經      yǐjīng     Adv   already
4  覺得      juéde      Vst   to feel; to think
5  以前      yǐqián     N     before; in the past
6  過        guò        Ptc   (experiential marker: have done before)
7  幾        jǐ         Det   a few; several
8  放        fàng       V     to put; to place
9  客廳      kètīng     N     living room
10 還        hái        Adv   even (more); still

Key Phrases
1  雖然…，可是…  —  我的房間雖然不大，可是我很喜歡。  —  Full concession pair; 雖然 can sit before or after the subject, 可是 always starts the second clause.
2  A 比 B + Adj + 多了。  —  那台電視機比舊的大多了。  —  A comparison with a *large* gap; 多了 is what makes it "much more", not just "more".
3  很久沒 + V + 了。  —  我已經很久沒看電視了。  —  "Haven't done X in ages" — the 了 is required to mark the ongoing gap.
4  我覺得…最…  —  我覺得坐在沙發上看書最舒服。  —  Mark an opinion as yours rather than a fact, then rank it with 最.
5  A 比 B + 還 + Adj。  —  我房間的窗戶比客廳的還大。  —  Use 還 when B was already impressive and A beats it anyway.
