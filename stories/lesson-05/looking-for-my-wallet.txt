《我的錢包在哪裡？》Where Is My Wallet?
Situation: Zhongming can't find his wallet before going out for afternoon tea with Youmei; his mom suggests places to check while she is busy cooking, and he finally finds it under his book.
Source: Lesson 5 – 我的錢包在哪裡？Where Is My Wallet? (Dialogue 2), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1) — the book's dialogue, verbatim
═══════════════════
(Textbook Dialogue 2; pinyin and English are copied from the provided
source packet.)

[Turn 1 — 中明站在房間門口，肩上背著包，一邊摸空口袋一邊對客廳叫媽媽]
中明：媽，我跟友美要去喝下午茶，可是我的錢包不在房間裡。
Mā, wǒ gēn Yǒuměi yào qù hē xiàwǔ chá, kěshì wǒ de qiánbāo bú zài fángjiān lǐ.
Mom, Youmei and I are going to have afternoon tea now, but my wallet is not in the bedroom.

[Turn 2 — 媽媽站在客廳桌子旁，指著桌上的一個錢包]
媽媽：客廳的桌子上有一個錢包，是不是你的？
Kètīng de zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge qiánbāo, shì bú shì nǐ de?
In the living room, there is a wallet on the table. Is that yours?

[Turn 3 — 中明拿起桌上的錢包看一看，搖頭說不是自己的]
中明：那不是我的，是哥哥的。我的錢包不在客廳裡。
Nà bú shì wǒ de, shì gēge de. Wǒ de qiánbāo bú zài kètīng lǐ.
It is not mine. It is my elder brother's. Mine is not in the living room.

[Turn 4 — 媽媽看向沙發，手往低處比，提醒中明再找一個地方]
媽媽：沙發下面呢？
Shāfā xiàmiàn ne?
How about checking under the sofa?

[Turn 5 — 中明跪在沙發旁邊，一手伸到沙發下面找，抬頭求媽媽幫忙]
中明：沙發上面、下面跟旁邊都沒有。媽，妳幫我找，好嗎？
Shāfā shàngmiàn, xiàmiàn gēn pángbiān dōu méi yǒu. Mā, nǐ bāng wǒ zhǎo, hǎo ma?
It is neither on the sofa, under the sofa, nor beside the sofa. Mom, could you find it for me?

[Turn 6 — 媽媽站在廚房門口，穿著圍裙拿著鍋，忙著要去做飯]
媽媽：我很忙，現在要去廚房做飯，你再去房間找吧。
Wǒ hěn máng, xiànzài yào qù chúfáng zuòfàn, nǐ zài qù fángjiān zhǎo ba.
I am very busy and I am going to the kitchen to cook now. You go check your room again.

[Turn 7 — （中明再去房間）中明回到房間書桌前，拿起一本書，看見錢包在書下面]
中明：啊！在這裡！錢包在我的書下面。
Ā! Zài zhèlǐ! Qiánbāo zài wǒ de shū xiàmiàn.
Ah! There it is! The wallet is under my book.

Vocabulary
1  跟      gēn        Conj  and
2  可是    kěshì      Conj  but
3  房間    fángjiān   N     room
4  裡(面)  lǐ(miàn)   N     inside; in
5  客廳    kètīng     N     living room
6  桌子    zhuōzi     N     table
7  上(面)  shàng(miàn) N    above; over; on top of
8  有      yǒu        Vst   there is/are; to exist
9  哥哥    gēge       N     elder brother
10 沙發    shāfā      N     sofa
11 下(面)  xià(miàn)  N     below; under; underneath
12 旁邊    pángbiān   N     side; by the side of; next to
13 幫      bāng       V     to help
14 找      zhǎo       V     to look for
15 廚房    chúfáng    N     kitchen
16 再      zài        Adv   again

Key Phrases
1  X 不在 [place] 裡。  —  我的錢包不在房間裡。  —  Report where something is not when you are looking for it.
2  [Place] 上有 [thing]，是不是你的？  —  客廳的桌子上有一個錢包，是不是你的？  —  Point something out and check ownership with 是不是.
3  那不是我的，是 X 的。  —  那不是我的，是哥哥的。  —  Correct a wrong guess about whose thing it is.
4  A、B 跟 C 都沒有。  —  沙發上面、下面跟旁邊都沒有。  —  List several places and say none of them has the thing.
5  妳幫我 + V，好嗎？  —  媽，妳幫我找，好嗎？  —  Ask for a favor politely with 幫我.

MEDIUM (A1-A2) — same beats, richer word choice
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — 中明站在房間門口，肩上背著包，一邊摸空口袋一邊對客廳叫媽媽]
中明：媽，我跟友美兩點半要去喝下午茶，可是我的錢包不見了，房間裡到處都沒有。
Mā, wǒ gēn Yǒuměi liǎng diǎn bàn yào qù hē xiàwǔ chá, kěshì wǒ de qiánbāo bú jiàn le, fángjiān lǐ dàochù dōu méiyǒu.
Mom, Youmei and I are going for afternoon tea at two thirty, but my wallet has disappeared; it's nowhere in my room.

[Turn 2 — 媽媽站在客廳桌子旁，指著桌上的一個錢包]
媽媽：我記得客廳的桌子上有一個錢包，那個是不是你的？
Wǒ jìde kètīng de zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge qiánbāo, nà ge shì bú shì nǐ de?
I remember there is a wallet on the living room table; is that one yours?

[Turn 3 — 中明拿起桌上的錢包看一看，搖頭說不是自己的]
中明：那個不是我的，是哥哥的。客廳我每個地方都看了，真的沒有。
Nà ge bú shì wǒ de, shì gēge de. Kètīng wǒ měi ge dìfāng dōu kàn le, zhēn de méiyǒu.
That one is not mine; it is my brother's. I looked everywhere in the living room, and it really is not there.

[Turn 4 — 媽媽看向沙發，手往低處比，提醒中明再找一個地方]
媽媽：沙發下面呢？你找了沒有？
Shāfā xiàmiàn ne? Nǐ zhǎo le méiyǒu?
What about under the sofa? Have you looked there?

[Turn 5 — 中明跪在沙發旁邊，一手伸到沙發下面找，抬頭求媽媽幫忙]
中明：沙發上面、下面跟旁邊我都找了，還是沒有。媽，妳幫幫我，好不好？
Shāfā shàngmiàn, xiàmiàn gēn pángbiān wǒ dōu zhǎo le, háishì méiyǒu. Mā, nǐ bāngbang wǒ, hǎo bù hǎo?
I looked on the sofa, under it, and beside it, but still nothing. Mom, help me a little, okay?

[Turn 6 — 媽媽站在廚房門口，穿著圍裙拿著鍋，忙著要去做飯]
媽媽：我現在很忙，快要去廚房做飯了，你自己再去房間找一次吧。
Wǒ xiànzài hěn máng, kuài yào qù chúfáng zuòfàn le, nǐ zìjǐ zài qù fángjiān zhǎo yí cì ba.
I am busy right now and am about to go cook in the kitchen. Go look in your room one more time yourself.

[Turn 7 — （中明再去房間）中明回到房間書桌前，拿起一本書，看見錢包在書下面]
中明：啊！在這裡！原來錢包在我的書下面。
Ā! Zài zhèlǐ! Yuánlái qiánbāo zài wǒ de shū xiàmiàn.
Ah! Here it is! So the wallet was under my book.

Vocabulary
1  兩點半    liǎng diǎn bàn N    two thirty
2  不見了    bú jiàn le      V-Ptc to have gone missing
3  到處      dàochù          Adv  everywhere
4  記得      jìde            Vst  to remember
5  那個      nà ge           Det  that one
6  每個      měi ge          Det  every
7  地方      dìfāng          N    place; spot
8  真的      zhēn de         Adv  really
9  還是      háishì          Adv  still
10 幫幫      bāngbang        V    to help a little
11 快要...了 kuài yào...le   Ph   to be about to
12 自己      zìjǐ            N    oneself; by oneself
13 一次      yí cì           N-M  once; one time
14 原來      yuánlái         Adv  so it turns out

Key Phrases
1  [Thing] 不見了。  —  我的錢包不見了。  —  Say something has gone missing.
2  [Place] 裡到處都沒有。  —  房間裡到處都沒有。  —  Say you searched a whole space and found nothing.
3  我記得...  —  我記得客廳的桌子上有一個錢包。  —  Introduce something you remember, while keeping the tone soft.
4  每個 + N + 都 + V 了。  —  客廳我每個地方都看了。  —  Say every place or item in a set has been checked.
5  快要 + V + 了。  —  快要去廚房做飯了。  —  Say something is about to happen soon.
6  再 V 一次。  —  你自己再去房間找一次吧。  —  Ask someone to try one more time.

HARD (A2) — same beats, fullest word choice
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — 中明站在房間門口，肩上背著包，一邊摸空口袋一邊對客廳叫媽媽]
中明：媽，我跟友美兩點半要去喝下午茶，可是我找了半天還是沒有，我快遲到了。
Mā, wǒ gēn Yǒuměi liǎng diǎn bàn yào qù hē xiàwǔ chá, kěshì wǒ zhǎo le bàntiān háishì méiyǒu, wǒ kuài chídào le.
Mom, Youmei and I are going for afternoon tea at two thirty, but I have searched for a long time and still do not have it; I am going to be late.

[Turn 2 — 媽媽站在客廳桌子旁，指著桌上的一個錢包]
媽媽：我剛剛看過，客廳的桌子上有一個錢包，那個是不是你的？
Wǒ gānggāng kàn guò, kètīng de zhuōzi shàng yǒu yí ge qiánbāo, nà ge shì bú shì nǐ de?
I saw one just now; there is a wallet on the living room table. Is that one yours?

[Turn 3 — 中明拿起桌上的錢包看一看，搖頭說不是自己的]
中明：那個比我的大一點，是哥哥的。客廳我也找過了，真的沒有。
Nà ge bǐ wǒ de dà yìdiǎn, shì gēge de. Kètīng wǒ yě zhǎo guò le, zhēn de méiyǒu.
That one is a little bigger than mine; it is my brother's. I have looked in the living room too, and it really is not there.

[Turn 4 — 媽媽看向沙發，手往低處比，提醒中明再找一個地方]
媽媽：沙發下面呢？那裡最容易掉東西了。
Shāfā xiàmiàn ne? Nàlǐ zuì róngyì diào dōngxi le.
What about under the sofa? Things fall there most easily.

[Turn 5 — 中明跪在沙發旁邊，一手伸到沙發下面找，抬頭求媽媽幫忙]
中明：沙發上面、下面跟旁邊我都找過了。媽，如果妳有空，幫我一起找，好嗎？
Shāfā shàngmiàn, xiàmiàn gēn pángbiān wǒ dōu zhǎo guò le. Mā, rúguǒ nǐ yǒu kòng, bāng wǒ yìqǐ zhǎo, hǎo ma?
I have already looked on the sofa, under it, and beside it. Mom, if you have time, help me look together, okay?

[Turn 6 — 媽媽站在廚房門口，穿著圍裙拿著鍋，忙著要去做飯]
媽媽：我今天真的沒空，現在要去廚房做飯，你再去房間仔細找一次吧。
Wǒ jīntiān zhēn de méi kòng, xiànzài yào qù chúfáng zuòfàn, nǐ zài qù fángjiān zǐxì zhǎo yí cì ba.
I really do not have time today, and I am going to the kitchen to cook now. Go look carefully in your room one more time.

[Turn 7 — （中明再去房間）中明回到房間書桌前，拿起一本書，看見錢包在書下面]
中明：啊！找到了！原來錢包一直在我的書下面。
Ā! Zhǎodào le! Yuánlái qiánbāo yìzhí zài wǒ de shū xiàmiàn.
Ah! Found it! So the wallet was under my book the whole time.

Vocabulary
1  半天      bàntiān    N     a long while
2  遲到      chídào     V     to be late
3  剛剛      gānggāng   Adv   just now
4  過        guò        Ptc   experiential marker
5  比        bǐ         Prep  compared to; than
6  大一點    dà yìdiǎn  Ph    a little bigger
7  容易      róngyì     Vs    easy; likely to
8  掉        diào       V     to drop; to fall
9  東西      dōngxi     N     thing; stuff
10 如果      rúguǒ      Conj  if
11 有空／沒空 yǒu kòng / méi kòng Vs to have / not have free time
12 一起      yìqǐ       Adv   together
13 仔細      zǐxì       Adv   carefully; thoroughly
14 一直      yìzhí      Adv   all along; continuously

Key Phrases
1  V 了半天，還是...  —  我找了半天還是沒有。  —  Say you tried for a long time but the result did not change.
2  V 過了。  —  客廳我也找過了。  —  Report that you have already checked or tried something.
3  A 比 B + Adj + 一點。  —  那個比我的大一點。  —  Compare two similar things with a small difference.
4  最容易 + V + 了。  —  那裡最容易掉東西了。  —  Say where something is most likely to happen.
5  如果...，...  —  如果妳有空，幫我一起找，好嗎？  —  Add a polite condition before making a request.
6  原來...一直...  —  原來錢包一直在我的書下面。  —  Express a realization that something was true all along.
