《喜歡一個新牌子》Liking a New Brand
Situation: Yiwen compliments Yuanzhen's skirt, learns it is from a famous clothing brand, asks where to buy that brand, and worries about sizing before they agree to go shopping together.
Source: Lesson 8 — 這條裙子真好看 (Dialogue 1), mode: book
9 turns — one per line of the book's dialogue, exactly as the book has them.

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's Dialogue 1 verbatim from the source packet; pinyin
and English are the book's own companion lines.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: In a bright classroom after class, Yiwen notices Yuanzhen's skirt and smiles while pointing lightly toward the hem]
宜文：元真，妳的這條裙子真好看，我很喜歡。
Yuánzhēn, nǐ de zhè tiáo qúnzi zhēn hǎokàn, wǒ hěn xǐhuān.
Yuanzhen, your skirt is very beautiful; I like it very much.

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks down at the skirt, touches the fabric with one hand, and answers warmly]
元真：謝謝，這是我去年的生日禮物。
Xièxie, zhè shì wǒ qùnián de shēngrì lǐwù.
Thanks, this is my birthday gift from last year.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen leans closer with interest, holding her backpack strap and talking about the brand]
宜文：聽說這個牌子的東西很貴，可是很多人喜歡。
Tīngshuō zhè ge páizi de dōngxi hěn guì, kěshì hěn duō rén xǐhuān.
I heard that this clothing brand is very expensive, but a lot of people like it.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods and gestures toward her skirt, then toward a simple clothing display poster on the classroom wall]
元真：是啊，因為這個牌子很有名，衣服、褲子和裙子也都很好看，所以很多人買。
Shì ā, yīnwèi zhè ge páizi hěn yǒumíng, yīfu, kùzi hé qúnzi yě dōu hěn hǎokàn, suǒyǐ hěn duō rén mǎi.
Yes, it is. This brand is very famous and its tops, pants and skirts are very beautiful, so many people buy it.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Yiwen takes out her phone as if ready to search for the store, asking where the brand is sold]
宜文：妳知道去哪裡買這個牌子的衣服嗎？
Nǐ zhīdào qù nǎlǐ mǎi zhè ge páizi de yīfu ma?
Do you know where you can get this clothing brand?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen points toward a department-store shopping bag beside her chair, looking confident]
元真：知道，百貨公司都有，不難找。
Zhīdào, bǎihuògōngsī dōu yǒu, bù nán zhǎo.
Yes. I think you can get them in department stores; it is not too difficult to find.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Yiwen looks at the skirt again, then glances down at herself with a worried face and one hand near her waist]
宜文：我很想去看，可是我最近胖了五公斤，我怕衣服不好買。
Wǒ hěn xiǎng qù kàn, kěshì wǒ zuìjìn pàng le wǔ gōngjīn, wǒ pà yīfu bù hǎo mǎi.
I would like to check it out. But I have recently gained 5 kilograms, so I am worried that they don't have my size.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen gives Yiwen a reassuring smile and lifts her phone, ready to go shopping together]
元真：妳胖了嗎？我不覺得啊，我跟妳一起去看。我也想去買今年夏天的新衣服。
Nǐ pàng le ma? Wǒ bù juéde ā, wǒ gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù kàn. Wǒ yě xiǎng qù mǎi jīnnián xiàtiān de xīn yīfu.
Have you gained weight? I don't think so. I can go check with you. I would like to buy the new summer collections.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Yiwen relaxes and nods, holding her phone calendar while both friends smile about making a plan]
宜文：好，我們找時間一起去。
Hǎo, wǒmen zhǎo shíjiān yìqǐ qù.
Okay, let's make time for it.

Vocabulary
1  裙子        qúnzi        N      skirt
2  去年        qùnián       N      last year
   年          nián         N/M    year
   今年        jīnnián      N      this year
   明年        míngnián     N      next year
3  牌子        páizi        N      brand
4  因為        yīnwèi       Conj   because
5  有名        yǒumíng      Vs     famous
6  褲子        kùzi         N      pants
7  所以        suǒyǐ        Conj   therefore; so
   為什麼      wèishénme    Adv    why
8  百貨公司    bǎihuògōngsī N      department store
9  難          nán          Vs     difficult; hard
   容易        róngyì       Vs     easy
10 最近        zuìjìn       Adv    recently; lately
11 胖          pàng         Vs     fat
   瘦          shòu         Vs     thin
   高          gāo          Vs     tall
   矮          ǎi           Vs     short
12 公斤        gōngjīn      M      kilogram
   斤          jīn          M      catty (=0.6 kilogram in Taiwan)
13 怕          pà           Vst    to be scared of; to be afraid of
14 跟          gēn          Prep   with
15 時間        shíjiān      N      time

Key Phrases
1  [item] 真好看 — 妳的這條裙子真好看，我很喜歡。 — Use this to compliment clothing or another visible item.
2  因為...，所以... — 因為這個牌子很有名，衣服、褲子和裙子也都很好看，所以很多人買。 — Give a reason, then the result.
3  知道去哪裡...嗎？ — 妳知道去哪裡買這個牌子的衣服嗎？ — Ask whether someone knows where to buy or find something.
4  跟 + person + 一起去 — 我跟妳一起去看。 — Suggest doing an activity together.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: In a bright classroom after class, Yiwen notices Yuanzhen's skirt and smiles while pointing lightly toward the hem]
宜文：元真，你的這條裙子真好看，顏色也很適合你，我真的很喜歡。
Yuánzhēn, nǐ de zhè tiáo qúnzi zhēn hǎokàn, yánsè yě hěn shìhé nǐ, wǒ zhēnde hěn xǐhuān.
Yuanzhen, your skirt is really pretty. The color also suits you; I really like it.

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks down at the skirt, touches the fabric with one hand, and answers warmly]
元真：謝謝，這是我去年生日的禮物，是我姊姊送的。
Xièxie, zhè shì wǒ qùnián shēngrì de lǐwù, shì wǒ jiějie sòng de.
Thanks. This is my birthday gift from last year; my older sister gave it to me.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen leans closer with interest, holding her backpack strap and talking about the brand]
宜文：聽說這個牌子的衣服比較貴，可是同學都說很好看。
Tīngshuō zhè ge páizi de yīfu bǐjiào guì, kěshì tóngxué dōu shuō hěn hǎokàn.
I heard this brand's clothes are comparatively expensive, but classmates all say they look great.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods and gestures toward her skirt, then toward a simple clothing display poster on the classroom wall]
元真：是啊，因為這個牌子很有名，衣服、褲子和裙子都好看，所以很多人買。
Shì ā, yīnwèi zhè ge páizi hěn yǒumíng, yīfu, kùzi hé qúnzi dōu hǎokàn, suǒyǐ hěn duō rén mǎi.
Yes. Because this brand is famous, its tops, pants, and skirts all look good, so many people buy it.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Yiwen takes out her phone as if ready to search for the store, asking where the brand is sold]
宜文：你知道哪一家百貨公司可以買這個牌子的衣服嗎？
Nǐ zhīdào nǎ yì jiā bǎihuògōngsī kěyǐ mǎi zhè ge páizi de yīfu ma?
Do you know which department store sells this brand's clothes?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen points toward a department-store shopping bag beside her chair, looking confident]
元真：知道，車站附近的百貨公司都有，不太難找。
Zhīdào, chēzhàn fùjìn de bǎihuògōngsī dōu yǒu, bù tài nán zhǎo.
Yes. Department stores near the station all have them; they are not too hard to find.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Yiwen looks at the skirt again, then glances down at herself with a worried face and one hand near her waist]
宜文：我很想去看看，可是最近胖了五公斤，怕沒有合適的尺寸。
Wǒ hěn xiǎng qù kànkan, kěshì zuìjìn pàng le wǔ gōngjīn, pà méiyǒu héshì de chǐcùn.
I really want to go look, but I recently gained five kilograms, so I'm afraid there won't be a suitable size.

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen gives Yiwen a reassuring smile and lifts her phone, ready to go shopping together]
元真：你胖了嗎？我不覺得。我跟你一起去看，也想買今年夏天的新衣服。
Nǐ pàng le ma? Wǒ bù juéde. Wǒ gēn nǐ yìqǐ qù kàn, yě xiǎng mǎi jīnnián xiàtiān de xīn yīfu.
Have you gained weight? I don't think so. I'll go look with you, and I also want to buy new clothes for this summer.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Yiwen relaxes and nods, holding her phone calendar while both friends smile about making a plan]
宜文：好，我們下課以後找時間一起去。
Hǎo, wǒmen xiàkè yǐhòu zhǎo shíjiān yìqǐ qù.
Okay, after class let's find a time to go together.

Vocabulary
1  顏色        yánsè        N      color
2  適合        shìhé        Vst    to suit; to fit
3  真的        zhēnde       Adv    really
4  姊姊        jiějie       N      older sister
5  比較        bǐjiào       Adv    comparatively; more
6  同學        tóngxué      N      classmate
7  哪一家      nǎ yì jiā    Det-M  which one (store/company)
8  車站        chēzhàn      N      station
9  附近        fùjìn        N      nearby area
10 不太        bù tài       Adv    not too; not very
11 合適        héshì        Vs     suitable
12 尺寸        chǐcùn       N      size
13 下課        xiàkè        V-sep  to finish class
14 以後        yǐhòu        N      after; later

Key Phrases
1  [item] 也很適合你 — 你的這條裙子真好看，顏色也很適合你，我真的很喜歡。 — Add a more personal compliment after saying something looks good.
2  聽說...，可是... — 聽說這個牌子的衣服比較貴，可是同學都說很好看。 — Report what you heard, then add a contrast.
3  哪一家 [store] 可以買...? — 你知道哪一家百貨公司可以買這個牌子的衣服嗎？ — Ask for a specific place to buy something.
4  [event] 以後找時間一起去 — 好，我們下課以後找時間一起去。 — Make a simple plan after another activity ends.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: In a bright classroom after class, Yiwen notices Yuanzhen's skirt and smiles while pointing lightly toward the hem]
宜文：元真，這條裙子的剪裁比我想的還好看，很適合你，我越看越喜歡。
Yuánzhēn, zhè tiáo qúnzi de jiǎncái bǐ wǒ xiǎng de hái hǎokàn, hěn shìhé nǐ, wǒ yuè kàn yuè xǐhuān.
Yuanzhen, the cut of this skirt looks even better than I expected. It suits you, and the more I look, the more I like it.

[Turn 2 — scene note: Yuanzhen looks down at the skirt, touches the fabric with one hand, and answers warmly]
元真：謝謝，這是我去年生日收到的禮物，我一直很喜歡。
Xièxie, zhè shì wǒ qùnián shēngrì shōudào de lǐwù, wǒ yìzhí hěn xǐhuān.
Thanks. This is a gift I received for my birthday last year, and I have always liked it.

[Turn 3 — scene note: Yiwen leans closer with interest, holding her backpack strap and talking about the brand]
宜文：我聽說這個牌子的東西雖然很貴，可是樣子不錯，所以很多人喜歡。
Wǒ tīngshuō zhè ge páizi de dōngxi suīrán hěn guì, kěshì yàngzi bùcuò, suǒyǐ hěn duō rén xǐhuān.
I heard that although this brand's things are expensive, the styles are good, so many people like them.

[Turn 4 — scene note: Yuanzhen nods and gestures toward her skirt, then toward a simple clothing display poster on the classroom wall]
元真：對，因為這個牌子很有名，衣服、褲子、裙子的樣子都好看，所以常常賣得很快。
Duì, yīnwèi zhè ge páizi hěn yǒumíng, yīfu, kùzi, qúnzi de yàngzi dōu hǎokàn, suǒyǐ chángcháng mài de hěn kuài.
Right. Because this brand is famous, the tops, pants, and skirts all look good, so they often sell quickly.

[Turn 5 — scene note: Yiwen takes out her phone as if ready to search for the store, asking where the brand is sold]
宜文：那你知道哪一家百貨公司比較容易買到這個牌子的衣服嗎？
Nà nǐ zhīdào nǎ yì jiā bǎihuògōngsī bǐjiào róngyì mǎi dào zhè ge páizi de yīfu ma?
Then do you know which department store is more likely to have this brand's clothes?

[Turn 6 — scene note: Yuanzhen points toward a department-store shopping bag beside her chair, looking confident]
元真：知道，車站旁邊那家百貨公司應該有，找起來不會太難。
Zhīdào, chēzhàn pángbiān nà jiā bǎihuògōngsī yīnggāi yǒu, zhǎo qǐlái bú huì tài nán.
Yes. The department store next to the station should have them; looking for them won't be too hard.

[Turn 7 — scene note: Yiwen looks at the skirt again, then glances down at herself with a worried face and one hand near her waist]
宜文：我想先試穿看看，可是最近胖了五公斤，如果尺寸不合怎麼辦？
Wǒ xiǎng xiān shìchuān kànkan, kěshì zuìjìn pàng le wǔ gōngjīn, rúguǒ chǐcùn bù hé zěnme bàn?
I want to try them on first, but I recently gained five kilograms. What if the size doesn't fit?

[Turn 8 — scene note: Yuanzhen gives Yiwen a reassuring smile and lifts her phone, ready to go shopping together]
元真：你看起來沒有胖，我陪你去試；我也正想買今年夏天的新衣服。
Nǐ kàn qǐlái méiyǒu pàng, wǒ péi nǐ qù shì; wǒ yě zhèng xiǎng mǎi jīnnián xiàtiān de xīn yīfu.
You don't look like you've gained weight. I'll go try things on with you; I was also just thinking of buying new summer clothes.

[Turn 9 — scene note: Yiwen relaxes and nods, holding her phone calendar while both friends smile about making a plan]
宜文：好，那我們這週找個時間一起去逛。
Hǎo, nà wǒmen zhè zhōu zhǎo ge shíjiān yìqǐ qù guàng.
Okay, then let's find a time this week to go shopping together.

Key Phrases
1  A 比 B 還 Adj — 這條裙子的剪裁比我想的還好看，很適合你，我越看越喜歡。 — Say something exceeds what you expected.
2  越 V 越 Adj — 這條裙子的剪裁比我想的還好看，很適合你，我越看越喜歡。 — Describe a feeling that grows as an action continues.
3  雖然...，可是...，所以... — 我聽說這個牌子的東西雖然很貴，可是樣子不錯，所以很多人喜歡。 — Balance a drawback with a reason people still like something.
4  找起來不會太難 — 車站旁邊那家百貨公司應該有，找起來不會太難。 — Reassure someone that a search should be manageable.
5  如果...怎麼辦？ — 我想先試穿看看，可是最近胖了五公斤，如果尺寸不合怎麼辦？ — Raise a practical worry before making a plan.
