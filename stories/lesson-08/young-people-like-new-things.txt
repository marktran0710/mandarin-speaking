《年輕人喜歡新東西》Young People Like the New Trend
Situation: A narrator reflects on young people browsing new clothes, bags, and cars, then explains why looking is fun but buying is not always necessary.
Source: Lesson 8 – 這條裙子真好看 (短文 Reading, track 08-3), mode: book

═══════════════════
EASY (A1)
═══════════════════
(This tier is the textbook's 短文《年輕人喜歡新東西》verbatim; pinyin and English
are the book's own, from the source packet.)

[Turn 1 — scene note: inside a bright department store, young women look at racks of new clothes, shelves of shoes, and purse displays while the narrator observes nearby]
Narrator：百貨公司裡有很多衣服、鞋子和皮包，都是最新的，所以很多年輕女生喜歡到百貨公司去，漂亮的、流行的衣服，她們都想穿穿看。
Bǎihuògōngsī lǐ yǒu hěn duō yīfu, xiézi hé píbāo, dōu shì zuì xīn de, suǒyǐ hěn duō niánqīng nǚshēng xǐhuān dào bǎihuògōngsī qù, piàoliàng de, liúxíng de yīfu, tāmen dōu xiǎng chuānchuān kàn.
Clothes, shoes and purses in department stores are all the latest; therefore, many young ladies like to go there and try on both beautiful and trendy items.

[Turn 2 — scene note: in a bookstore magazine area, young men smile while reading car magazines beside a display of shiny new car pictures]
Narrator：很多年輕男生愛在書店看汽車雜誌，汽車很貴，他們不一定可以買新車，可是都看得很開心。
Hěn duō niánqīng nánshēng ài zài shūdiàn kàn qìchē zázhì, qìchē hěn guì, tāmen bù yídìng kěyǐ mǎi xīn chē, kěshì dōu kàn de hěn kāixīn.
Many young men like to read car magazines at bookstores. Cars are expensive and they don't usually have the money to buy new ones; however, they are very happy just by reading those magazines.

[Turn 3 — scene note: the narrator stands between an old useful backpack on a small table and a shop window with new clothes and car models, holding a modest wallet]
Narrator：我覺得有的舊東西很不錯，有的新東西也很有趣，我很喜歡看新車、新衣服，可是因為我的錢不多，所以我不常買。
Wǒ juéde yǒude jiù dōngxi hěn búcuò, yǒude xīn dōngxi yě hěn yǒuqù, wǒ hěn xǐhuān kàn xīn chē, xīn yīfu, kěshì yīnwèi wǒ de qián bù duō, suǒyǐ wǒ bù cháng mǎi.
I think some old stuff is good and some new ones are interesting. I like to check the new car, new clothes out, but I do not have much money, so I don't buy them often.

Vocabulary
1  年輕      niánqīng    Vs    young
2  皮包      píbāo       N     wallet; purse
3  女生      nǚshēng     N     girl; female student
4  男生      nánshēng    N     boy; male student
5  雜誌      zázhì       N     magazine
6  舊        jiù         Vs    old; used

Key Phrases
1  [Place] 裡有 [things]。  —  百貨公司裡有很多衣服、鞋子和皮包。  —  Say what a place has inside it.
2  都是最新的  —  都是最新的。  —  Use 都是 to say a whole group belongs to the same category.
3  喜歡到 [place] 去  —  很多年輕女生喜歡到百貨公司去。  —  Say where someone likes to go.
4  愛在 [place] 看 [thing]  —  很多年輕男生愛在書店看汽車雜誌。  —  Name a place and the thing someone likes to read or look at there.
5  因為...，所以...  —  可是因為我的錢不多，所以我不常買。  —  Give a reason and its result.

MEDIUM (A1-A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: inside a bright department store, young women look at racks of new clothes, shelves of shoes, and purse displays while the narrator observes nearby]
Narrator：百貨公司裡有各種衣服、鞋子和皮包，差不多都是最新的，所以很多年輕女生一下課就喜歡去逛，看到漂亮、流行的衣服，也想試穿看看。
Bǎihuògōngsī lǐ yǒu gèzhǒng yīfu, xiézi hé píbāo, chàbuduō dōu shì zuì xīn de, suǒyǐ hěn duō niánqīng nǚshēng yí xiàkè jiù xǐhuān qù guàng, kàn dào piàoliàng, liúxíng de yīfu, yě xiǎng shìchuān kànkan.
Department stores have all kinds of clothes, shoes, and purses, and almost all of them are the newest, so many young women like to go browse as soon as class ends; when they see pretty, trendy clothes, they also want to try them on.

[Turn 2 — scene note: in a bookstore magazine area, young men smile while reading car magazines beside a display of shiny new car pictures]
Narrator：很多年輕男生常在書店看汽車雜誌，雖然汽車很貴，他們不一定可以買新車，可是只看一看也覺得很開心。
Hěn duō niánqīng nánshēng cháng zài shūdiàn kàn qìchē zázhì, suīrán qìchē hěn guì, tāmen bù yídìng kěyǐ mǎi xīn chē, kěshì zhǐ kàn yi kàn yě juéde hěn kāixīn.
Many young men often read car magazines at bookstores. Although cars are expensive and they cannot necessarily buy a new car, they still feel happy just looking.

[Turn 3 — scene note: the narrator stands between an old useful backpack on a small table and a shop window with new clothes and car models, holding a modest wallet]
Narrator：我覺得有些舊東西很不錯，有些新東西也很有趣；我喜歡看新車和新衣服，可是因為錢不多，所以只是偶爾買。
Wǒ juéde yǒuxiē jiù dōngxi hěn búcuò, yǒuxiē xīn dōngxi yě hěn yǒuqù; wǒ xǐhuān kàn xīn chē hé xīn yīfu, kěshì yīnwèi qián bù duō, suǒyǐ zhǐshì ǒu'ěr mǎi.
I think some old things are pretty good, and some new things are interesting too. I like looking at new cars and new clothes, but because I do not have much money, I only buy them once in a while.

Vocabulary
1  各種      gèzhǒng     Det   all kinds of; various
2  差不多    chàbuduō    Adv   almost; about the same
3  下課      xiàkè       VO    to finish class
4  逛        guàng       V     to stroll; to browse
5  看到      kàn dào     V     to see
6  試穿      shìchuān    V     to try on clothes
7  雖然      suīrán      Conj  although
8  有些      yǒuxiē      Det   some
9  只是      zhǐshì      Adv   only; merely
10 偶爾      ǒu'ěr       Adv   occasionally

Key Phrases
1  一 [event] 就...  —  很多年輕女生一下課就喜歡去逛。  —  Say someone does something right after another event.
2  看到 [thing]，也想...  —  看到漂亮、流行的衣服，也想試穿看看。  —  Connect seeing something with wanting to try it.
3  雖然...，可是...  —  雖然汽車很貴，他們不一定可以買新車，可是只看一看也覺得很開心。  —  Admit one fact, then give the contrasting result.
4  有些...，有些...  —  有些舊東西很不錯，有些新東西也很有趣。  —  Compare two groups without saying one is always better.

HARD (A2)
═══════════════════

[Turn 1 — scene note: inside a bright department store, young women look at racks of new clothes, shelves of shoes, and purse displays while the narrator observes nearby]
Narrator：百貨公司裡的新衣服、鞋子和皮包一樣比一樣流行，所以不少年輕女生一有時間就去逛；看到好看的款式，她們總想先試穿看看。
Bǎihuògōngsī lǐ de xīn yīfu, xiézi hé píbāo yíyàng bǐ yíyàng liúxíng, suǒyǐ bù shǎo niánqīng nǚshēng yì yǒu shíjiān jiù qù guàng; kàn dào hǎokàn de kuǎnshì, tāmen zǒng xiǎng xiān shìchuān kànkan.
The new clothes, shoes, and purses in department stores are each trendier than the last, so quite a few young women go browsing whenever they have time. When they see a nice-looking style, they always want to try it on first.

[Turn 2 — scene note: in a bookstore magazine area, young men smile while reading car magazines beside a display of shiny new car pictures]
Narrator：很多年輕男生雖然知道汽車很貴，不一定有錢買新車，可是只要在書店看過幾本汽車雜誌，就覺得很開心。
Hěn duō niánqīng nánshēng suīrán zhīdào qìchē hěn guì, bù yídìng yǒu qián mǎi xīn chē, kěshì zhǐyào zài shūdiàn kàn guò jǐ běn qìchē zázhì, jiù juéde hěn kāixīn.
Although many young men know cars are expensive and they may not have the money to buy a new car, as long as they have read a few car magazines at a bookstore, they feel happy.

[Turn 3 — scene note: the narrator stands between an old useful backpack on a small table and a shop window with new clothes and car models, holding a modest wallet]
Narrator：比起什麼都買新的，我更喜歡先看看、想想；舊東西有時候很實用，新車、新衣服也很有趣，可是錢不多，所以我不常買。
Bǐqǐ shénme dōu mǎi xīn de, wǒ gèng xǐhuān xiān kànkan, xiǎngxiang; jiù dōngxi yǒu shíhòu hěn shíyòng, xīn chē, xīn yīfu yě hěn yǒuqù, kěshì qián bù duō, suǒyǐ wǒ bù cháng mǎi.
Compared with buying everything new, I prefer to look and think first. Old things are sometimes very useful, and new cars and new clothes are interesting too, but I do not have much money, so I do not buy them often.

Vocabulary
1  一樣比一樣  yíyàng bǐ yíyàng  phrase each one more ... than the last
2  不少        bù shǎo            Det    quite a few
3  有時間      yǒu shíjiān        Vs     to have time
4  款式        kuǎnshì            N      style; design
5  總          zǒng               Adv    always
6  知道        zhīdào             Vst    to know
7  只要        zhǐyào             Conj   as long as
8  過          guò                Ptc    experiential marker
9  本          běn                M      measure word for books/magazines
10 比起        bǐqǐ               Prep   compared with
11 更          gèng               Adv    even more; more
12 實用        shíyòng            Vs     practical; useful

Key Phrases
1  一樣比一樣 Adj  —  新衣服、鞋子和皮包一樣比一樣流行。  —  Say that each item in a group feels more strongly like the adjective.
2  一有 [time/chance] 就...  —  不少年輕女生一有時間就去逛。  —  Say someone does something whenever the chance appears.
3  雖然...，可是只要...，就...  —  雖然知道汽車很貴，不一定有錢買新車，可是只要在書店看過幾本汽車雜誌，就覺得很開心。  —  Balance a limitation with the small condition that is enough.
4  比起 A，我更喜歡 B  —  比起什麼都買新的，我更喜歡先看看、想想。  —  Compare two choices and state the one you prefer.
5  有時候...，也...  —  舊東西有時候很實用，新車、新衣服也很有趣。  —  Keep two sides of an opinion in balance.
