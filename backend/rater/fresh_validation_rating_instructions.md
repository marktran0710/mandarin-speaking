# Rating instructions — Mandarin tone acceptability

Thank you for helping evaluate a Mandarin pronunciation practice tool.

## What you will hear

Short recordings of single syllables produced by learners of Mandarin. For each
one you will see the **target character**, its **expected pinyin**, and the
**expected tone**. You will hear the learner's attempt.

## The question

> **Is the target tone acceptable enough for the learner to continue without
> being asked to repeat it?**
>
> **YES** — acceptable, the learner can move on
> **NO** — the learner should try again

Please judge the **tone** specifically. Consonant or vowel imperfections should
not by themselves make you answer NO unless they make the tone impossible to
judge.

This is not "was it perfect". It is the practical teaching question: would you
let this attempt pass and move on, or ask for another try?

## Then

- **Confidence (1–5)** — 1 = very unsure, 5 = very sure.
- **Perceived tone (optional)** — which tone you actually heard: T1 / T2 / T3 /
  T4 / uncertain. Please answer "uncertain" freely; a guess is less useful than
  an honest uncertainty.
- **Comment (optional)** — anything notable.

## Please note

- You will **not** see what the system decided. That is deliberate.
- You will **not** see the other rater's judgements, and please do not discuss
  the recordings with them until rating is complete.
- Some recordings appear more than once. This is an intentional consistency
  check. Please judge each as you hear it and do not try to recall an earlier
  answer.
- Recordings appear in a shuffled order, so consecutive items are usually from
  different learners.
- Work in whatever sittings suit you, but please avoid rating while tired — take
  a break rather than pushing through.

There is no right answer we are hoping for. If most attempts sound acceptable to
you, or most do not, either is a useful result.
