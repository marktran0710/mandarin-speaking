# Item review packet for a Mandarin teacher

You do not need to read the research protocol to complete this. Everything
needed is on this page.

## What we are asking you to do

We are testing a practice tool that listens to a learner say a single Mandarin
character and either confirms the tone sounds acceptable or asks them to try
again. Before we record any learners, we need a Mandarin teacher to confirm the
**16 characters below are reasonable things to ask a beginner-to-intermediate
learner of Mandarin to say aloud, one at a time**.

We checked each character in the Taiwan MoE dictionary and kept only characters
with a single reading, so the expected tone is never in doubt. **That check does
not tell us whether the character is suitable for a learner, or natural to say
on its own.** That is the judgement we need from you.

For each item, please tick the four boxes if you agree, add any comment, and
mark **APPROVE** or **REPLACE**. Record your decisions on
`data/fresh_validation_item_teacher_review_TEMPLATE.csv`, or on a printout of
this page — whichever is easier.

The four checkboxes are:

| box | question |
|---|---|
| **A** | Appropriate for a CFL learner at beginner–intermediate level? |
| **B** | Is the expected reading unambiguous as presented here? |
| **C** | Is it natural to say this character on its own? |
| **D** | Is the difficulty reasonable — not unusually hard or unusually easy? |

### Question 3 — Tone 3 acceptability rule

Four items are Tone 3. Said on their own, some speakers produce the full dipping
contour and others a low or "half-third" realisation, and both are commonly
heard. The people who later judge the recordings need to be told, in advance,
which of these counts as acceptable — otherwise each will decide privately and
the results will reflect their disagreement rather than the learners'
pronunciation.

Proposed wording, **for your approval, amendment, or rejection** — please do not
treat it as already decided:

> A naturally produced low / half-third realisation may be rated ACCEPTABLE when
> it is appropriate for the elicitation context and does not require a canonical
> full dipping contour.
