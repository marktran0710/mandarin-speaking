# Taking part — what to expect

Thank you for helping with this study. It takes about 15–20 minutes.

## What you will do

You will see a Mandarin character on the screen, with its pinyin and its
meaning. Say it out loud, in your normal speaking voice, into the microphone.

There are **16 characters**, one at a time.

## What the app will tell you

After each recording the app will show you one of two messages:

> **"Your tone sounds acceptable. You can continue."**

> **"I'm not confident enough to confirm this attempt. Please try once more."**

**The second message does not mean your pronunciation was wrong.** It means the
app was not confident enough to confirm it. This app is deliberately cautious:
it only confirms when it is quite sure, so it will often ask you to repeat
something you said perfectly well. If that happens several times, it is telling
you about the app, not about you.

The app will never tell you that you were incorrect, and it will never tell you
which tone it thought you said.

## Repeating

Sometimes you will be asked to try again. Please just say the character again
naturally. You do not need to exaggerate or over-pronounce it — say it the way
you would normally say it.

## Recording

Your voice is recorded. If you have given consent, the recordings will be used
for research on how well this kind of practice tool works. Later, Mandarin
teachers may listen to the recordings to judge the pronunciation. They will not
know who you are.

## Taking part is voluntary

- You do not have to take part.
- You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and without any
  consequence.
- You can ask for your recordings to be removed, following the withdrawal
  procedure in the consent form.
- If a question or a recording makes you uncomfortable, tell the researcher.

The consent form explains exactly how the recordings are stored, how long they
are kept, and who can access them. Please read it before starting and ask about
anything that is unclear.

## At the end

There is a short questionnaire — about 6 questions — asking how clear the
messages were and how the session felt. There are no right answers, and
criticism is genuinely useful.

## What this study is for

We are checking how well this practice tool works. **We do not yet know whether
it works well** — that is exactly what this study is for. Please do not take its
messages as a judgement of your Mandarin.

If you have any questions, ask the researcher before you begin.
